# Watch subscribed venues' reviews weekly and alert on a critical one

## Goal
Every Monday the reviews of WON prospects' venues are scraped for the last ten days; a new review of three stars or fewer gets an e-mail to the owner within the week with the text and a reply draft, and the portal's Reviews tab shows "needs a reply" at the top.

## Why
A monthly report tells the owner about a one-star review four weeks late. The weekly scrape is nearly free for a few dozen venues, and "we told you within days" is the part of the subscription an owner will mention to the next owner.

## Scope
- In: a weekly cron `review-watch` (per active city) calling `GoogleReviewService.scrapeMonth`-like logic with `reviewsStartDate` ten days back, analysis of the new reviews, a `review-alert` persisted event and worker mailing the prospect's contact (template in `packages/emails`, with the draft from `ReviewReplyAgent`), `GoogleReview.alertedAt` so a review alerts once.
- Out: alerts for non-subscribed venues, SMS.

## Pointers
- `apps/backend/src/modules/venues/services/google-review.service.ts`, `modules/pipeline/crons/monthly-pipeline.cron.ts` (cron pattern), `modules/email/services/email.service.ts`, `modules/outreach/entities/prospect.entity.ts` (`stage`, `contactEmail`).

## Acceptance
- [x] PGlite (mail queue and Apify mocked): a new 2-star review alerts once; a 5-star one does not; a venue whose prospect is not WON is not scraped. (`pipeline/tests/review-watch.pglite.spec.ts`; never run against Apify.)

## Open questions
