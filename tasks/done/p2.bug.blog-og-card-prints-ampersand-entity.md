# The blog's share card prints "&amp;" for an "&" in a title or excerpt

## Goal
An article titled "Reviews & reels" shows "Reviews & reels" on its `og.png`, not "Reviews &amp; reels".

## Why
Svelte escapes `&` in text when it renders the card component, and satori prints the entity as it comes. Every link preview of such an article shows the entity.

## Scope
- In: `apps/frontend/src/routes/(blog)/blog/[articleId]/og.png/+server.ts` renders `BlogOgImage` with `svelte/server` itself, decodes `&amp;`, turns `&lt;` into a look-alike, and passes the string to `ImageResponse`, the way `routes/(highlights)/[city=city]/[month=month]/[card=card].png/+server.ts` does (`cardMarkup`). Consider moving that helper to `lib/` once two routes use it.
- Out: the card's layout.

## Pointers
- `apps/frontend/src/routes/(highlights)/[city=city]/[month=month]/[card=card].png/+server.ts` (`cardMarkup`)
- `seo-and-ssr` skill, the satori entity rule

## Acceptance
- [x] A rendered `og.png` of an article with "&" and "<" in its title shows "&" and "‹", checked by rendering the route's `GET` with a stubbed fetch.
