# Keep own accounts (VenueAccount) on the brand, not the venue

## Goal
A brand's own TikTok and Instagram accounts belong to the brand, several per brand (the user: "own accounts can be multiple per brand"), one optionally a branch's own: `BrandAccount` (and its snapshots) is keyed by brand, detected from the brand's posts, and the report and portal read the brand's (a branch's only in its city).

## Why
A chain has one Instagram (`@burgermeister`), not one per branch. Since `brand-as-unit`, accounts sit on each report's described branch (`PipelineService` `SCRAPE_OWN_ACCOUNTS`, `VenueAccountService.detect`), so a brand whose described branch changes loses its account history, and the prospect's `instagram` is mapped onto that branch.

## Scope
- In: `VenueAccount.venue` → `brand` (rename the entity if it reads better), a hand-written migration moving rows to `venues.brand_id` (dedupe by platform and handle), `detect` / `addProspectHandles` / `scrapeMonth` / `forReport` / `snapshots` by brand, `/admin/venues/:id/accounts` → the brand's, the venue page's Accounts panel and the brand page.
- Out: nothing left out.
