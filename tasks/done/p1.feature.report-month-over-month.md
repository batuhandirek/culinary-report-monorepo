# Show how the month compares with the one before, and follow up on last month's actions

## Goal
The KPI band shows the change against the previous month (views, posts by others, creators, reviews, stars this month, reply rate); the highlights model sees the previous month's figures and, when a previous report exists, its actions, so it can write "reply rate went from 10 % to 40 % after last month's suggestion".

## Why
Without "better or worse than last month" the report is a snapshot. The trend and the follow-through on the actions are what make the next month's report worth paying for. The cost is nil: `summarizeReport` is pure, so the previous month's numbers need no LLM call, and previous `Report` rows already hold the actions.

## Scope
- In:
  - `ReportData.previous`: the previous month's `social` and `reviews` KPIs (from `summarizeReport` over that month's mentions and reviews) and, when a `Report` exists for it, its `highlights.actions`. `undefined` when the venue has no data for that month at all (first report), so the view shows no deltas rather than zeros.
  - `ReportView` KPIs: delta with direction and the previous value in the note.
  - Highlights prompt: `previousMonth` in the input and an instruction to compare, and to say what happened to each previous action when actions are given.
- Out: a six-month chart; the Google rating trend line (task `report-rating-trend`).
