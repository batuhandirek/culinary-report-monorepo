# Add TripAdvisor reviews for the tourist side of the story

## Goal
Report venues that exist on TripAdvisor get their month's TripAdvisor reviews scraped and analysed with the same `ReviewAnalysisAgent`; the report's review section shows Google and TripAdvisor side by side and names where tourists and locals disagree.

## Why
Google mixes locals and tourists; TripAdvisor is mostly visitors. For a Mitte or Kreuzberg venue the gap between the two (tourists praise the vibe, locals complain about price) is a finding of its own, and a second rating line matters for venues that live on tourists. Cost is negligible.

## Scope
- In: `Venue.tripadvisorUrl` (found once by a name + city search through the actor, confirmed with `isSameVenue`), a `platform` column on `GoogleReview` → rename the entity to `Review` with `platform: google | tripadvisor` (the DTOs follow; nothing is released), the scrape in `SCRAPE_REVIEWS`, `ReportData.reviews.byPlatform`, a small block in the report and a filter in the portal explorer.
- Out: Yelp (thin in Germany), Facebook reviews.

## Pointers

- `apps/backend/src/modules/venues/entities/google-review.entity.ts`, `services/google-review.service.ts`, `util/venue-names.ts` (`isSameVenue`).
