# Name the creators who drove the month, and those who covered the neighbours but not the venue

## Goal
A report section lists the top creators by views (handle, followers, platform, visited and paid flags, views, link) and "creators active in your district this month who did not post about you" (handle, followers, which neighbours they covered), as invitation candidates. The actions can name one.

## Why
Owners act on names: whom to thank, whom to invite. The post cards show six posts, not creators, and the neighbour query already finds the district's talked-about venues, so the creators behind them are one join away.

## Scope
- In: `ReportData.creators` (top eight by views, one row per author, with their posts' flags) and `ReportData.creatorsNearby` (top eight by followers among authors with at least one non-promotional post about a district neighbour this month and none about the venue, listing the neighbours they covered); the section; a prompt hint ("an action may suggest inviting a nearby creator by handle").
- Out: contacting creators, storing creator profiles.

## Pointers
- `apps/backend/src/modules/reports/services/report.service.ts` (`neighbours` SQL), `util/summarize-report.ts`.
- `apps/frontend/src/views/report/ReportView.svelte`.

## Acceptance
- [x] A rebuilt dev report shows both lists; a venue without a district shows only the first.
- [x] Unit test on the two selections.

None.
