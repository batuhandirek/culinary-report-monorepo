# Dish name grouping

## Goal
"kebab" (463 reviews on dev), "doner kebab" (378), "döner" (104), "doner" (47) and "kebabs" (32) are one row in the city's dish table; "burgers" (274) and "burger" (247) are one row everywhere. A venue's report keeps dishes apart where they are different dishes (dürüm is not döner for a kebab shop).

## Why
`dishKey` is `plain(name)`: accents and case only, so the trend tables and the report's dish list split one dish over spellings and plurals, and the shares under-count every dish that has two names. The food-topic index already knows the aliases ("gemuse kebab", "durum", "iskender" → kebab; "hamburger", "smash burger" → burgers).

## Design
- `dishKey`: `plain(name)` with English plurals folded per word (`ies` → `y`, a trailing `s` dropped from words of 4+ letters not ending in `ss`, `us`, `is`), so "burgers" = "burger", "fries" = "fry", "cookies" = "cookie". The label stays the most common spelling (`topLabel` in trends; the first seen in the report). `itemsForDish` (delivery) uses `plain` directly and is unaffected.
- City tables (`compute-trends.ts`): a dish name the index names as a whole (`FoodTopicIndex.exact`) as a `dish` or `drink` topic groups under the topic's key and label ("Döner & kebab"); the rest under `dishKey`. The venue report and `pricePosition` keep `dishKey` only.
- Every count stays once per source.
