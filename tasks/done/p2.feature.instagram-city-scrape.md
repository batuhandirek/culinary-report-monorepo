# Find venues on Instagram city-wide, not only for venues TikTok already found

## Goal
The monthly run also scrapes Instagram by Berlin food hashtags within its own budget, so a venue that is big on Instagram but absent from TikTok can enter the top list and get a report.

## Why
Instagram is only scraped per top venue (`SCRAPE_VENUE_SOCIAL`), and the top list comes from TikTok mentions, so the pipeline is TikTok-biased: brunch, café and bakery crowds skew Instagram, and those are prospects (Café YOHAKU, Love at first bite, Die Zimtschnecke).

## Scope
- In: evaluate Apify Instagram hashtag actors on cost per 1k posts and on whether items carry caption, tagged location and play counts (`apify/instagram-hashtag-scraper` first), a `fromInstagramHashtag` item shape in `SocialPostService`, `SocialScrapeService.scrapeCityInstagram`, a `SCRAPE_INSTAGRAM` step after `SCRAPE_TIKTOK` with its own default budget, an Instagram hashtag list next to the TikTok ones.
- Out: Instagram comments, Reels search.
