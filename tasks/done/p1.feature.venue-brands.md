# Group a chain's branches under one brand, find all of them, and score the brand once

## Goal
A chain (Brammibal's Donuts, Nhas City Chicken, Burgermeister) is one `Brand`, with its branches as ordinary `Venue`s under it, in one city or several. A brand search on Google Maps finds every branch in a city. A post that names the brand without saying which branch counts for the brand, not for whichever branch matching happened to pick. The lead score ranks the brand once.

## Why
Chains with an owner who decides are good prospects: they have budget, and a question a single café doesn't have ("which branch does better, and why?"). `PROSPECTS.md` already ranks Nhas City Chicken #2 and Brammibal's "next in line". But each branch is an unrelated venue today, so a chain's numbers are wrong (dev DB, 2026-10-18):
- **Most branches are missing.** `lookupNames` keeps `LOOKUP_RESULTS_PER_NAME` (3) results and never searches a name again (`VenueLookup`; "burgermeister" and "brammibal s" were looked up on 2026-10-15 with 3 results each). We have Brammibal's ×3 and Burgermeister ×2. One `compass/crawler-google-places` search per name (`locationQuery` Berlin, `maxCrawledPlacesPerSearch` 40, $0.21 for both) returned 9 Brammibal's and 15 "Burgermeister …" restaurants. Its tail is look-alikes (Peter Pane, Golt'z Burger, Burger Vision, Dunkin', SammyS) and "Burgermeister Produktion" (Grocery store). A post naming "Brammibal's, Maybachufer 8" stayed `none` because that branch wasn't a venue.
- **All the buzz lands on one branch.** Without a location hint, matching picks one branch. Nhas City Chicken: Mitte 19 mentions, Neukölln 0, Charlottenburg 0. Brammibal's: all 7 on one Mitte venue. Burgermeister: all 3 on Pankow.
- **The lead score splits the brand.** Nhas 91.3 / 31.5 / 31.5; Brammibal's 63.7 / 31.5 / 31.5; Burgermeister 46.5 / 31.5.
- **It's common.** 96 names sit on 2+ open venues in Berlin, and that's before brand searches add the missing branches.

The follow-up `brand-report` (report, outreach and portal per brand) builds on this.

## Scope
- In:
  - **`Brand` entity**: `name`, `normalizedName` (unique), `ownership` `independent` | `corporate` (the admin's call), `confirmedAt`, `branchesSearchedAt` per city. A nullable `Venue.brand`. A brand is not tied to a city: its branches are, through `Venue.city`.
  - **Suggestions**: venues of a city sharing `nameHead` (`venue-lead-score.service.ts`, today only a namesake count) are suggested as a brand. Names with a suffix group too ("Brammibal's Donuts (Prenzlauer Berg)", "Burgermeister Leopoldplatz"). Same name head in another city: offered as the same brand, never joined by itself. An admin confirms (picking `ownership`), edits the branch list or dismisses. Nothing becomes a brand on its own ("suggestions are never enabled by themselves").
  - **Branch search** (`VenueScrapeService.searchBrand(brand, city, budgetUsd)`): one `compass/crawler-google-places` run per brand × city: `searchStringsArray: [brand.name]`, `locationQuery` the city, `maxCrawledPlacesPerSearch` `BRAND_SEARCH_RESULTS` (40), reviews and images off. Every item is saved like a lookup (`saveGooglePlace`, source lookup, so not an opening). Items whose name matches the brand's name head and that are food venues (`isFoodVenue`) are offered as branches; look-alikes aren't. It skips `VenueLookup`'s never-twice rule, which still holds for unmatched-mention lookups. Runs on confirm and from the brand page ("Find branches"), and again with each city's quarterly `SCRAPE_VENUES` for confirmed brands, so new branches show up.
  - **Matching** (`social/services/mention-matching.service.ts`): when the best candidates are branches of one confirmed brand and the hint names no branch's district or street, the mention is brand-level (brand set, no venue). A hint that names a branch still goes to that branch. Matching stays inside the post's city: a brand-level mention is for the brand's branches in that city. Admin corrections (`manual` / `ignored`) stay final.
  - **Counts**: every query that reads mentions by venue counts brand-level mentions for the brand: `topVenueIds`, the lead score, trends (matched mentions). List each query joining `mentions.venue_id` and decide it in the log.
  - **Lead score**: one score per brand in a city (written on each of its branches there). `independent` comes from `Brand.ownership` (independent 1, corporate 0), with no branch limit. Venues not in a confirmed brand keep today's rule (`LEAD_CHAIN_NAMESAKES`). The deny-list stays for the big chains.
  - **Admin UI**: a Brands list (suggested / confirmed, branches per city), a brand page (branches by city, "Find branches" with the offered places to tick, ownership), a Brand panel on the venue page.
- Out:
  - The brand's report, prospect, outreach and portal (`brand-report`).
  - Reviews stay per branch (Google reviews belong to a place).
  - Branches in cities we don't cover (no `City` row): no posts are scraped there, so nothing to report.
