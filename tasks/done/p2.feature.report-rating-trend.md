# Show the Google rating trend in the report, refreshed every month

## Goal
Every report venue (and its competitors) gets a `VenueSnapshot` during the monthly run, and the report shows the all-time rating and review count as a small trend over the last six months.

## Why
`VenueSnapshot` rows are written only by `saveGooglePlace` (the `SCRAPE_VENUES` and `LOOKUP_VENUES` steps), which the monthly run does not do for existing venues, so a venue found in June shows June's rating in its October report, and there is no trend to draw. Owners watch their Google rating more than anything else.

## Scope
- In: check whether `compass/Google-Maps-Reviews-Scraper` items carry the place's `totalScore` and `reviewsCount`; if so write the month's snapshot from `scrapeMonth`, otherwise run `compass/crawler-google-places` by `placeIds` for the report venues inside the `SCRAPE_REVIEWS` budget; `ReportData.venue.ratingTrend` (last six snapshots); a sparkline next to the "Stars" KPI.
- Out: daily snapshots.

## Pointers
- `apps/backend/src/modules/venues/services/google-review.service.ts`, `services/venue.service.ts` (snapshot logic in `saveGooglePlace`), `entities/venue-snapshot.entity.ts`.
- `apps/frontend/src/views/report/ReportView.svelte`.
