# Show competitors' and the district's booked-out share next to the venue's, in the report and the portal

## Goal
The report's "Did the buzz fill the place?" section and the portal's Trends tab put the venue's booked-out share beside its picked competitors' and beside the district median, and the highlights agent may say "your Friday and Saturday evenings were 70 % booked, your competitors' 45 %, the district's 38 %". Busyness is compared as a trend, never as a level.

## Why
A booked-out share is the one demand figure that is comparable between venues: it is the share of 18:00–22:00 start times with no table for two, the same question asked of every venue. Google's busyness index is not (Google scales each histogram to the venue's own peak, see the skill), so competitors' busyness can only be shown as their change against the month before. Once `reserve-with-google-open-tables` samples the whole city, competitors and the district have samples for free; today only won prospects do, so nothing can be compared.

## Scope
- In:
  - `ReportCompetitor` gains `demand?: { booked: number; evenings: number }` (the month's mean over its sampled evenings, absent without samples) and `busyness?: { index: number; previousIndex?: number }` (its month's mean index and the month before, absent without samples with popular times; the report shows the change, not the level).
  - `ReportDemand` gains `district?: { median: number; venues: number }` and `city?: { median: number; venues: number }`: the median booked-out share over every venue sampled in the district / the city for the same evenings, with the count of venues behind it, absent below 5 venues. Computed in `ReportSignalsService` from `VenueReservationSample` joined to `venues.district` / `city_id` (one SQL, not a per-venue loop).
  - `ReportBusyness.svelte`: the "Weekend tables" tile gets the competitor and district figures under the venue's; the weekly grid stays the venue's own. The competitor table on the report (`ReportView.svelte`, where competitors are drawn) gets a "Weekend tables" column (booked-out share) and a "Foot traffic" column (busyness change in points, "+6" / "−3", blank when unknown).
  - Highlights prompt (`report-highlights.agent.ts`): a sentence on `weekendTables` (venue, competitors, district medians; compare only where all three exist) and on competitors' busyness change; `highlightsInput` in `report.service.ts` carries them.
  - Portal series: `PortalSeriesCompetitorOutputDto` gains `booked?` per month (from `VenueReservationSample`, the same way `addBusyness` reads `VenueBusyness`), `PortalSeriesMonthOutputDto` gains `booked?` for the venue and `districtBooked?`; the Trends tab's booked-out chart draws competitors as the faint lines it already uses for views and the district median as a dashed line.
  - `summarize-report` / `report-signals` tests and the skill's report and portal bullets.
- Out: sampling itself; busyness level comparisons between venues; owner-picked competitors (they already flow through `ReportData.competitors`).
