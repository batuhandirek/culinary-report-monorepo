# Let the extraction flag "just opened" mentions, and use them as the earliest opening signal

## Goal
A mention carries `isNewOpening` when the post says the place is new ("just opened", "new in Neukölln", "soft opening"), and a venue first matched from such a mention within the last three months counts as opened for the lead score's new-place bonus, the Trends page's openings panel and the public "new in the city" content, a year before the city list can tell.

## Why
`VenueStatusChange` `opened` is written only by a city-wide Google list after the city's first one; Berlin's baseline was 15 October 2026, so nothing is "new" until the January 2027 list, and even then only for places Google's capped slice happens to return. Creators post about a place in its first weeks: the post is the earliest signal there is, and the extraction already reads every post.

## Scope
- In: a boolean in `MentionExtractionAgent`'s schema and prompt ("only when the post itself says the place is new or recently opened"), stored on `Mention`; `VenueStatusChangeService.newVenueIds` and the lead score's `LEAD_NEW_PLACE_*` check also accepting "a matched mention flagged as new within `LEAD_NEW_PLACE_MONTHS`, venue under `LEAD_NEW_PLACE_MAX_REVIEWS` reviews"; the Trends openings panel listing them with the source "posts".
- Out: writing a `VenueStatusChange` row from a post (the row means "a scrape saw it"); the public page itself (`monthly-city-highlights-page`).
