# Let a brand's report cover all its cities at once

## Goal
A brand with branches in several covered cities can get one monthly report across all of them (`Brand.reportScope: 'all'`), instead of one per city (the default, what ships today).

## Why
A chain owner with branches in Berlin and Hamburg may want one report and one invoice. Today each city's monthly run builds the brand's report on its branches in that city (`brand-as-unit`: reports are brand × city × month), so they get two.

## Scope
- In: `Brand.reportScope` (`city` | `all`, admin-set on the brand page). For `all`: one report with `city` null (decide), built after the last city's monthly run of the month has finished (the cron runs cities one after another, `MONTHLY_PIPELINE_CRON`). Buzz and reviews are summed across cities, and "Branch by branch" is grouped by city. Trends, the city section, search ranks and neighbourhood stay per city (each city's posts come from its own sources and budget) or are dropped for `all`. Outreach is already one prospect per brand.
- Out: cities we don't cover (no `City` row), since their posts are never scraped.
