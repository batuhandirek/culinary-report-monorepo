# A public monthly page per city: the most talked-about places, risers, creators, with share cards

## Goal
`/<city>/<month>` (e.g. `/berlin/2026-10`) is a public, server-rendered page an admin publishes on the 3rd: the ten brands most posted about by others that month (posts, views, creators), the five fastest risers, the most viewed post embedded with its creator credited, the top creators, the district with the most posts, and the dishes that rose. Three share images (1200×630, 1080×1080, 1080×1350) render from the same data for Instagram and links. Every listed venue is a warm lead.

## Why
It is the product's demo in public, from data the monthly run already computes (`topBrandIds`, `TrendService.month` rising venues and dishes, the posts), and the venues on it are the next month's prospects ("you were #4 in Berlin in October"). Marketing content from this data is the cheapest reach we have.

## Rules for the page (from the public content doc)
- Our own counts only: no Google ratings, no review quotes, nothing from OSM.
- Creators credited by handle with a link to the original; videos through the platforms' official embeds.
- Positive lists only (no "fastest falling").
- Published by an admin, never automatically; mention corrections apply before publishing; a city do-not-list for venues and creators who ask to be left out.

## Scope
- In: `CityHighlight` (city × month, the computed data as jsonb, `publishedAt`), `CityHighlightService.build(cityId, month)` and `publish` / `unpublish`; `GET /highlights/:citySlug/:month` (published only, 404 otherwise); an admin page under Trends (preview, publish, the do-not-list on the city); the SSR route with meta tags and JSON-LD, listed in the sitemap; the three share images from one renderer (the blog's `og.png` route is the pattern).
- Out: a newsletter; district guides and the creator leaderboard as pages of their own (later items of the same doc); anything automatic.

## Pointers
- `apps/backend/src/modules/trends/services/trend.service.ts` (`month`: rising venues, dishes, districts, creators), `apps/backend/src/modules/social/services/mention-matching.service.ts` (`topBrandIds`), `apps/backend/src/modules/overview/services/brand-list.service.ts` (the month's figures per brand)
