# Resolve menu items to food topics: what venues sell, at what price, per topic

## Goal
A `MenuItem` (Wolt / Lieferando, `SCRAPE_DELIVERY`) resolves to food topics on read, the same way a mention's cuisine does (`FoodTopicIndex.resolve` over its name, then its menu category), so a topic gains a **menu** side next to venues (supply), posts (buzz) and searches (demand): how many venues sell it, and at what median price, in the city and per district.

## Why
- **Price per kind of food** is the comparison an owner understands ("your ramen is €14.50, the district's median ramen is €12.90"). Today `pricePosition` compares a venue's median main course with its competitors' medians, mixing a burger with a salad, and matches the report's top dishes as raw words (`itemsForDish`).
- **Better venue tags**: a menu with twelve pho items says Vietnamese / pho more surely than Google's "Asian restaurant". It would be one more scored source in `venueTopics` (like posts by others), below the admin's tags.
- **Supply that posts miss**: "how many venues sell matcha, and is the price rising" is a trend the topic page can show without any extra scraping.

## Design (proposal)
- Never stored, like every other topic link: a new alias or topic retags menus at once. `resolveMenuItem(item)` in `food-topics/util/food-topics.ts` tries the item's name, then its category ("Ramen", "Bowls" → nothing), skipping drinks/sides/extras (`isMainCourse`) unless the topic is a drink.
- `MenuTopicService.prices(cityId, month, topicKeys?)`: per topic, venues with it on a menu, items, median and quartile price, by district at 5+ venues.
- Uses: topic page (a "On menus" number and price), `pricePosition` by the venue's primary topic first, then the overall median; `venueTopics` gets a `menu` source at `MIN_MENU_SHARE` of its main courses.

## Scope
- Out: scraping more menus (only the report venues and their competitors are scraped today, so menu figures lean to them — say so on the page, like dish shares do). Widening `SCRAPE_DELIVERY` to a city-wide sample is its own decision (cost).

## Google Maps menus
Google's place pages sometimes show a menu too. Today `Venue.details.menu` keeps only its link (detail pages only, `REFRESH_VENUE_DETAILS`), and no venue has one yet. Check what `compass/crawler-google-places` returns for a detail page with a menu (a link, or dish names and prices / "popular dishes"); if items come back, they are a second menu source for every report venue at no extra run, and cover places without Wolt or Lieferando.

## Google's review tags (stored since 2026-10-19)
Detail pages return `reviewsTags` ("people often mention", all-time counts: Döner Pro: vegan meat 12, dürüm 7, seitan 6; Choc-Spot: cookie 38, strawberries 10), now kept in `Venue.details.reviewsTags` for every venue `REFRESH_VENUE_DETAILS` covers. They resolve to food topics the same way as menu items and need no extra scrape: a cheaper first step than menus (which never came for lack of "Order online" links, see `popular-times-and-order-online-source`). Mind that tags mix dishes with staff and places ("employees", "station wagon"): only those resolving to a topic count.
