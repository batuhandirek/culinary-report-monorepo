# Tag venues with food topics and move cuisine consumers onto topics

## Goal
`VenueFoodTopic` (venue, topic, source `category` | `mentions` | `manual`, score, `primary`) recomputed monthly from Google categories and what posts call the venue; an admin's tag is final. Competitor suggestions, search-rank queries, the trends page's cuisine table, openings by food and the report's city section read topics instead of `normalizeCuisine` keys.

## Why
Six places derive a "kind of food" on the fly with their own rules; compounds ("vegan kebab") lose one side. Tags give competitors by shared topics (overlap, not one string), topic-level supply (venues per topic, openings) next to demand (searches) and buzz (mentions).

## Scope
- In: entity, the monthly recompute (a pipeline step, free), the venue page's tags, the consumers: `venue-competitor.service.ts`, `search-ranks.ts`, `compute-trends.ts`, `venue-status-change.service.ts`, `reports/util/city-section.ts`.
- Out: the public report.

## Log
- 2026-10-18: Decision: tags are worked out on read (categories + posts by others + the admin's rows), not stored by a monthly step: every other topic read resolves on the fly, a city takes milliseconds, a new alias retags at once, and no step means no run to orphan. Only `VenueFoodTopic` (an admin's tag or exclusion) is stored. Primary = first cuisine/dish of admin tags, main category, posts; else the first place type in that order. Competitors: `sharedFood` (3 same primary, 2 related through parents, 1 overlap; formats never food) + 0.5 per shared diet, replacing category/cuisine string matching.
