# Read the comments under the top posts: who wants to go, who disagrees, what they ask

## Goal
For each report venue, the comments under its top five posts of the month are scraped and classified: intent to visit ("saving this", "going Saturday"), confirmation ("been there, so good"), disagreement ("overrated", "they changed the recipe"), questions (where is it, how much, do they take cards), and tags of friends. The report shows "what viewers said": intent and disagreement counts, the top three questions, and an action when questions pile up ("people ask where you are: tag the location in your own posts").

## Why
Views say who watched; comments say who is coming and who was disappointed. A creator video with 300k views and 40 "where is this?" comments is a venue that is hard to find on the platform; "they changed the recipe" comments are the same early warning the review `changes` field catches. Cost is small: apidojo charges $0.30 per 1,000 comments, clockworks $0.50; 200 comments × 5 posts × 30 venues is about $10 a month.

## Scope
- In: `PostComment` entity (post, externalId, text, likes, `createdAt`, `intent` enum, `question` text or null, no author handle stored), `SocialScrapeService.scrapeComments(posts, perPost, budgetUsd)` through `ApifyService`, a `CommentIntentAgent` (batch of comments → intents, questions, a one-line summary of disagreements), a `SCRAPE_COMMENTS` + `ANALYSE_COMMENTS` step after `SCRAPE_VENUE_SOCIAL`, `ReportData.comments` and the section, the post page in the admin lists the comments.
- Out: replying to comments, Instagram comments (actor coverage is worse; a later task).
