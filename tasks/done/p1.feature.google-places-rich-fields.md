# Keep the Google Maps details that explain a venue: popular times, hours, amenities, "people also search"

## Goal
The Places scrape stores, per venue, the fields it throws away today: `popularTimesHistogram` and `popularTimesLivePercent`, `openingHours`, `additionalInfo` (service options such as outdoor seating, reservations, delivery, dine-in, payments, accessibility), `reviewsDistribution` (all-time 1–5 counts), `imagesCount`, `menu` link, `reserveTableUrl`, `orderBy` (delivery platforms), `temporarilyClosed`, `placesTags`, and `peopleAlsoSearch` (Google's own "similar places" list). Report venues and their competitors get these refreshed every month with `scrapePlaceDetailPage`.

## Why
Every correlation the user wants starts here. Popular times are the only free foot-traffic signal (task `busyness-index-from-popular-times`); `peopleAlsoSearch` is Google's competitor set, better than hand-picking (task `competitor-auto-suggest`); amenities split the city for fair comparison (outdoor vs not, delivery vs not); `reviewsDistribution` shows the all-time star shape next to the month's; `orderBy` tells which delivery platforms to scrape. The actor already returns all of it; `saveGooglePlace` drops it. Cost: detail pages are about $2 per 1,000 places on top of $1.50 per 1,000, so 60 venues a month is under $0.25.

## Scope
- In: `Venue.details` jsonb (typed interface `VenueDetails` with the fields above, dated `detailsAt`), `saveGooglePlace` writes it when present; a `REFRESH_VENUE_DETAILS` pipeline step (report venues ∪ competitors ∪ WON prospects, by `placeIds`, `scrapePlaceDetailPage: true`, `maxReviews: 0`) placed before `BUILD_REPORTS`; the admin venue overview shows hours, amenities, price, the star distribution and "people also search" as links; `ReportData.venue.amenities` and `reviewsDistribution` for the report cover.
- Out: images, questions and answers (personal data), the busyness time series (own task).
