# Record where the venue ranks on Google Maps for its category in its district

## Goal
Every month the pipeline runs the Google Maps searches that matter for each report venue ("ramen Mitte", "brunch Prenzlauer Berg", "döner Kreuzberg": category or cuisine × district, two or three per venue) and stores the venue's rank and the top ten. The report says "you rank 7th for 'ramen Mitte'; Cocolo ranks 2nd" and the trend over months.

## Why
Most guests find a place by searching the map, and the rank is a number the owner can move (reviews, photos, replies, hours). It is also the first metric that compares the venue with every neighbour at once, not only the picked competitors. Cost: one query returns twenty places for about three cents.

## Scope
- In: `VenueSearchRank` (venue, month, query, rank or null, top ten as `{ placeId, name, rating, reviewsCount }[]`), query generation from `category`/`categories` and the mention `cuisine` plus the district, a `TRACK_SEARCH_RANKS` step, `ReportData.searchRanks`, a report block, the admin venue page.
- Out: organic Google Search rank, paid local ads.
