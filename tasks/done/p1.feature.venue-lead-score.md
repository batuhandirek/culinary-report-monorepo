# Score every venue for outreach so the admin sees who to pitch

## Goal
Each venue carries a lead score (0–100) and the signals behind it, recomputed at the end of every monthly run. `/admin/venues` sorts and filters by it, the venue page shows the breakdown and what is missing to score it fully. It replaces the hand-made `PROSPECTS.md`.

## Why
Recruiting venues is the business right now: the user picks who gets a sample report. `PROSPECTS.md` was ranked by hand from the dev DB on four criteria (lives on social, already spends on it, has a problem we can name, independent with an owner who decides). All four are computable from what the pipeline already stores, and a stored score keeps the list current month after month instead of a one-off markdown file.

## Scope
- In:
  - `Venue.leadScore` (float, nullable, indexed) and `Venue.leadSignals` (jsonb: each signal's 0–1 value, the raw inputs behind it, and `missing: string[]` such as `reviews not scraped`). Migration.
  - `VenueLeadScoreService` with a pure, exported `scoreVenue(inputs)` and `scoreAll(month)` that computes inputs in a few SQL passes over the last **two** months (the report month and the one before).
  - Signals and weights, in one constant so they can be tuned:
    - **Buzz 0.35** — posts by others (own-account posts dropped with `isOwnAccount`), views, distinct creators. Use the venue's percentile among mentioned venues, not absolute numbers: scrape budgets vary month to month.
    - **Already spends 0.20** — has own-account posts, has promotional posts about it (`isAd || isPromotional`), has a website.
    - **Nameable problem 0.25** — complaint share of written reviews, unanswered critical reviews, reply rate < 50 %, `changes` noticed, negative or mixed mentions. Without scraped reviews this signal is *unknown*, not zero, and `missing` says so.
    - **Independent 0.10** — fewer than 4 venues share the normalized name head (`venueAliases`) and the name is not in a chain/institution deny-list constant (start from the "Left out" list in `PROSPECTS.md`).
    - **Reachable 0.10** — phone or website present; an own social account was seen.
    - **New-place bonus** — first seen (`createdAt`) within 6 months and under 300 Google reviews.
  - A `SCORE_LEADS` pipeline step, last in `MONTHLY_STEPS`, also startable from `/admin/pipeline`.
  - `/admin/venues`: a "Lead score" column (sortable, default sort stays mentions), a "Prospects only" toggle (score ≥ 60), the venue overview panel with the breakdown bars and the `missing` hints.
  - Delete `PROSPECTS.md` once the score reproduces most of its top 10. (Not done: see log; the coordinator decides.)
- Out: the sales pipeline itself (`modules/outreach` holds prospects, stages and follow-ups; task `prospects-from-lead-score` connects the score to it); the LLM pitch hook (task `prospect-pitch-hook`); any payment.

## Log

- 2026-10-17: Problem value = half the strongest part + half the mean of five parts (complaint share/0.4, unanswered critical/3, (0.5 − reply rate)/0.5, changes/2, negative-or-mixed post share/0.3), each capped at 1: one sharp problem is enough to pitch, several make it stronger.
