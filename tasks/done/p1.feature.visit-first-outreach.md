# Sell by visiting: a visit script, the visit's outcome and consent, a leave-behind, and a round planner

## Goal
The sales conversation can be run on foot. A salesperson plans a round of prospects by district with each place's address, phone and opening hours; takes a printed leave-behind (the report's cover, three findings and a QR code to its share link); logs the visit with what happened (who they spoke to, owner present or not, "come back on", got the e-mail and **consent to e-mail**); and the composer then knows it may e-mail the report. Everything hangs off the existing prospect, its touches and the share link.

## Why
Visits are the channel for Berlin's Imbisse, Döner and chicken places, cafés and bakeries (PROSPECTS.md): several have no website, none of the 21 prospects has an e-mail address, and German law needs consent before a cold e-mail (UWG §7). A visit yields that consent in person. Today `OutreachTouchKindEnum.VISIT` exists and counts as an outgoing touch (follow-ups 4 and 7 days later), but there is no visit template, no place to record the outcome, no leave-behind, and the sales list has no way to plan a walk.

## Scope
- In (first slice):
  - **Visit script** in `OUTREACH_TEMPLATES` (kind `VISIT`, en and de): the opener, the hook, the ask ("may I e-mail you the report?" / "can we sit down for ten minutes next week?"), what to leave behind. The composer treats it like the call script (notes field, logged as a `VISIT` touch).
  - **Outcome on the touch**: a `VISIT` or `CALL` touch carries `outcome` (`spoke_to_owner` | `spoke_to_staff` | `closed` | `nobody`), `comeBackAt` (sets the follow-up date and time: "the owner is in on Tuesdays after 15:00"), and `emailConsent` (true sets `Prospect.emailConsentAt`, which `EditProspectDialog` shows and can clear). The composer warns before an EMAIL touch to a prospect without consent and without a logged reply, and the report mail (`ReportDeliveryService`) stays as it is (won only).
  - **Leave-behind**: `GET /admin/reports/:id/leave-behind.pdf`, an A5 page printed by `ReportPdfService` from a new `/print/leave-behind` route: the cover title and headline, the three findings, the brand's name, a QR code to the share link (`ReportShareService.ensureShare`, so the scan is counted as an open) and the salesperson's contact; a "Print leave-behind" button on the Reports tab and the Sales tab.
- In (second slice, its own log line when started):
  - **Round planner** on the Brands page's Sales list: group by district, each row with street, phone, today's opening hours (`Venue.details.openingHours`) and the "come back" note; a "Plan a round" filter (stage, district, follow-up due) that prints as one list; the Today page lists today's planned visits (follow-ups due with a `VISIT` as the last touch).
- Out: maps and routing (a printed list with addresses is enough); the busyness-based "quiet hour to visit" until popular times return anything (`popular-times-and-order-online-source`); tracking visits by GPS.

## Open questions
- Should "spoke to the owner, said no" move the stage to lost automatically? Assumption: no, the stage stays the salesperson's move; the outcome is logged.
- QR code library: assumption, generate the SVG server-side with a small dependency (no network), embedded in the print page.
