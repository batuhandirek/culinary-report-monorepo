# Track openings and closures by district

## Goal
A quarterly city-wide Places refresh (and the monthly detail refresh for known venues) records when a venue first appears, closes temporarily or permanently, or reopens; the trends page shows openings and closures by district and category, and the report's neighbourhood section says "three places opened on your street this quarter, one closed".

## Why
New competition and closures are the context an owner reads a quiet month in; "new in the district" is also where tomorrow's prospects are (a new venue with a first wave of attention is the best lead in `PROSPECTS.md`).

## Scope
- In: `VenueStatusChange` (venue, `at`, kind: opened | temporarily_closed | permanently_closed | reopened, source step), written by `saveGooglePlace` on first sight and on flag flips, a `SCRAPE_VENUES` schedule of once a quarter per city (budget from the city row), the trends panel, `ReportData.neighbourhood.changes`, a "new venues" filter on the venues list feeding the lead score's new-place bonus.
- Out: opening dates before we first saw the venue.

## Pointers
- `apps/backend/src/modules/venues/services/venue.service.ts` (`saveGooglePlace`: `closed`), `venue-scrape.service.ts` (`scrapeCity`), `city-trends-page` task.

## Acceptance
- [x] PGlite: first sight writes `opened`; a flip to `permanentlyClosed: true` writes `permanently_closed` once.
