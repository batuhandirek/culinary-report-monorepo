# Portal charts: twelve months of views, posts, creators, reviews, stars, replies, topics and dishes

## Goal
The portal's Trends tab draws the venue's last twelve months: views and posts by others, creators, sentiment split, Google reviews and stars this month, reply rate, the all-time rating line, praise and complaint topic shares, the top dishes over time, and (when tracked) busyness and own-account followers. Each chart has the competitors as faint lines when picked, and a month tap opens that month's report.

## Why
This is the "explore and understand" the user asked for. The series are free: every month's `ReportData` is stored, so a series endpoint reads the stored reports and the snapshots, no recomputation and no LLM.

## Scope
- In: `GET /portal/venues/:id/series?months=12` (one row per month from `Report.data`: social, reviews, topic shares, dishes, `ratingTrend`, `busyness` when present, competitors' social and reviews from `data.competitors`), the Trends page with the dataviz skill's palette and rules (one system, light and dark), a month picker, empty states for months without a report.
- Out: custom date ranges, exports (a later task), the admin side (the admin can open the portal view).

## Pointers
- `apps/backend/src/modules/reports/entities/report.entity.ts` (`data`), `interfaces/report-data.interface.ts` (`previous`, `ratingTrend`, `competitors`).
- `busyness-index-from-popular-times` and `own-account-stats` tasks for the optional series.
- `.claude/skills/dataviz` (load before writing any chart).

## Acceptance
- [x] The series endpoint answers twelve rows for a dev venue with reports, nulls where a month has none.
- [x] The Trends tab renders every chart on a phone width with no horizontal scroll; competitors appear when picked.
