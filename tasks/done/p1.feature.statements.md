# Statements and their parts

**Read first**: the brief `statements-and-concepts` (the ELI5 table, Analysis §3 and §4, the five scenarios), then
this. Needs `topics-tree` and `terms` done. Program order: `topics-tree` → `terms` → **statements** →
`reread-sources-with-new-agents` → `google-tags-as-statements` → `talked-about-pages`.

## Goal
`Statement` (table `statements`) is one claim from one source about one subject: its English text, sentiment
(positive, negative, neutral, mixed, or null for not judged), a stated number with its unit, a noticed-change flag.
`StatementTopic` (table `statement_topics`) is its parts: one row per topic it is about, with its role (aspect |
food) and the term it came from. Reviews are read by agent A (claims), posts and articles by agent A (places and
claims, city claims included), every claim by agent B (parts), every wording by agent C (`terms`). Every reader of
`feedback_points` reads statements and shows the same counts for the same data; `feedback_points`, the four
feedback backlogs and the module `feedback` are gone (module `statements` in their place).

## Why
Today what a source says is fused to one venue, is always praise or complaint, and is judged by one big prompt per
source kind. "Fries for 10 euros" (no judgement) and "Berlin has increasing prices" (no venue) are lost; a prompt
change means re-extracting posts, which drops matches and corrections. The user (2026-10-29): one claim per row,
its parts as rows, three specialised agents, neutral facts and city claims kept.

## Scope
- In: the entities and migration (by SQL, no LLM), agents A (review; post/article) and B, the `statement-parts`
  backlog, the writers, every reader moved (list in the brief), DTOs, the frontend helpers and components, the
  factory, tests, the skill. The new extraction applies to sources analysed from now on.
- Out: re-reading stored sources (`reread-sources-with-new-agents`), Google tags (`google-tags-as-statements`),
  comments and press as sources (the columns exist; no writer yet), the designed admin UI and the "who talked about
  X" page (`talked-about-pages`).

## Entity changes (this phase only)
- New `Statement` (`statements`, serial id): `sourceKind` review | post | comment | press | google_tag; FKs
  `review` (cascade), `post` (cascade), `mention` (set null; the place a post's or comment's claim is about),
  `comment` (cascade), `webMention` (cascade), `venue` (cascade; a Google tag's) — check constraint: exactly one of
  review / post / comment / webMention / venue; `subject` place | city | none; `city` (cascade, the source's),
  `month` text nullable (null for an all-time source), `saidAt` timestamptz; `text` nullable, `sentiment` nullable,
  `change` boolean, `amount` double nullable, `unit` minutes | money nullable, `count` int default 1, `partsAt`
  timestamptz nullable (B's stamp: null = B's backlog). Indexes: (city, month), (post), (review), (mention),
  (venue, sourceKind).
- New `StatementTopic` (`statement_topics`): `statement` (cascade), `topic` (cascade), `role` aspect | food, `term`
  (set null), `rank`; unique statement × topic × role; index (topic, role).
- `Review.feedback` / `Mention.feedback` → `statements` collections (`SocialPost.statements` too: the city claims).
- Dropped: `FeedbackPoint`, `feedback_points`, the `feedback` module (its services, agents, controllers, DTOs move
  or go: `FeedbackService` → `StatementService`, `FeedbackThemeService` + `FeedbackDishService` +
  `FeedbackAmountService` → `StatementPartsService`, `DishNameService` already went in `terms`).

## Order of work
1. Entities + migration. The data part, SQL only: one statement per `feedback_points` row (review → `review`,
   `sourceKind review`, `subject place`; mention → `post` = the mention's post, `mention`, `sourceKind post`,
   `subject place`; `detail` → `text`; `sentiment` as is; `change`; `amount`, `unit`; `city`, `month`; `saidAt` =
   the review's `publishedAt` / the post's `postedAt`; `partsAt` = now for a row with a theme or no detail, null
   for an unfiled one), then parts: a row with a `theme` → one `aspect` part (the topic by key); a row with a
   `dishKey` → one `food` part per topic its term links (`term_topics`), `term` set, and a dish-only row with a
   term that has no link yet gets no part but keeps its `term` through… **note**: a dish-only statement needs its
   term even without a link, so `Statement.term` (set null) exists for "the wording this claim names" and the parts
   are derived from it; decide the column name (`objectTerm`) when writing the entity. Then drop `feedback_points`.
2. `StatementService` (the one writer): `replaceForReview(review, claims, …)`, `replaceForMention(mention, claims)`,
   `replaceCityClaims(post, claims)`, in the caller's unit of work as `replaceFor` is today; `statementsOf(source)`
   readers for DTOs; `waitComplaints` moved.
3. Agent A for reviews (`ReviewClaimsAgent`, mini, batches of 10 reviews, replacing `ReviewAnalysisAgent`): per
   review its overall sentiment and `claims: [{ text, change }]` — one thing said each, neutral facts kept, the
   dishes named listed as claims of their own when no claim names them (so dish counts hold); no theme, no dish, no
   amount here. Agent A for posts and articles (`PostClaimsAgent`, replacing `MentionExtractionAgent`): the places
   (today's mention fields: `venueName`, `locationHint`, `cuisine`, `city`, `visited`, `isNewOpening`, `sentiment`,
   `summary`; `isPromotional` on the post) and `claims: [{ text, about: <place index> | 'city' | 'none', change }]`.
   Both carry the POC rule (never drop a claim because it fits nothing) and the rules now in the prompts (only what
   the text says; English; transcripts misspell names).
4. Agent B (`ClaimPartsAgent`, mini, ~100 claims a call, any source): per claim `sentiment`, `aspect` (a key from
   the legend, checked against the index; unknown → left null, asked again next run), `foods: string[]` (the
   wordings as the source spells them, never translated or normalised; empty for a claim about the place), `amount`
   + `unit` (only a number the text states; the old `FEEDBACK_POINT_INSTRUCTION` and `parseAmount` rules), `change`.
   Input per claim: id, text, source kind, the venue's name, the dishes the source lists (so "crispy and juicy" can
   name the wings). `StatementPartsService.read({ limit })`: writes the aspect part, upserts each food wording as a
   term (`TermService.ensure`) and writes its food parts from the term's links, sets `partsAt`; runs inside
   `ANALYSE_REVIEWS` / `EXTRACT_MENTIONS` right after A over the step's new claims, and as the backlog
   `statement-parts` (priority 10) for any statement with `partsAt` null. `TermService.apply` writes food parts
   for statements whose term got links later (replacing the stopgap column).
5. Readers, one by one, each with its test: `summarize-report.ts` (`praiseOf` / `complaintsOf` / `changesOf` /
   `dishesOf` / `topicsOf` → `statements/util/statement-views.ts` over statements with parts), `countThemes`,
   `groupChanges`, `cityThemes` + `ReportCityService`, `editor-pack.ts`, `report-evidence.service.ts`,
   `city-theme-trend.tool.ts`, `report-signals.service.ts`, `reach.ts`, `report-observations.ts`,
   `report-playbooks.ts`, `trends/util/theme-counts.ts` + `theme-detail.ts` + `ThemeInsightService`,
   `TrendService` (`DISHES_OF`) + `compute-trends.ts`, `BrandLeadScoreService` (negative claims; changes),
   `landing-edition.service.ts` + `landing.ts`, `ReviewService.list` (topic filter) + `facets`, `PortalService`
   (reviews, posts), the admin DTOs (`StatementOutputDto` replacing `FeedbackPointOutputDto`: `text`, `sentiment`,
   `change`, `amount`, `unit`, `subject`, `parts: { role, topic: { key, label, kind }, term?: { key, name } }[]`),
   `report-data.interface.ts`. Rules kept: a source counts once per topic ("sources, not points"); own posts
   decided at read time; a `change` statement is no praise or complaint; brand and venue joined through the source.
6. Frontend: `lib/feedback.ts` → `lib/statements.ts` (`pointsOf`, `changesOf`, `topicsOf`, `dishesOf`, `talksAbout`,
   `sideOf`, `themeHref` over `parts`), `FeedbackNotes` → `StatementNotes` (neutral claims shown as "noted"),
   `TalksAbout`, `ReportMedia`, `TopicBars`, the Reviews tabs, the portal's `ReviewCard`, the post page.
7. Tests: `statements.pglite.spec.ts` (the five scenarios minus Google tags: the review "waited 50 minutes"; "damn
   good döner, but 15 euros" → two statements; a post's "damn good fries for 10 euros" neutral price claim; the blog's
   city claim and two place claims; a re-matched mention's statements following it; replaced on re-analysis; gone with
   the source; B's backlog; an unknown aspect key asked again), the moved readers' suites, `statement.factory.ts`
   (`addStatements`) replacing `feedback.factory.ts`; `npm run backend:test`.
8. Skill: the "What posts and reviews say is rows" and the themes/dish rules rewritten as statements, parts, A/B/C,
   two backlogs; the readers' list; the test list.
