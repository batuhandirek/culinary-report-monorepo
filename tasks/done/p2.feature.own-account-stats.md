# Track the venue's own TikTok and Instagram accounts month by month

## Goal
Each report venue has its own account handles stored (found by `isOwnAccount` on its posts, entered by the admin, or from `Prospect.instagram`) and a monthly `VenueAccountSnapshot` per account: followers, posts, average views and likes of the last ten posts, posting frequency. The report compares the venue's own reach with the creators' ("creators got 18× the views of your own account; your followers grew by 240").

## Why
"Already spends on it" is a lead signal and a report chapter: an owner who posts three times a week wants to know whether it works compared with one creator video. Profile scrapes are cheap (one item per profile) and follower growth over months is the simplest chart an owner understands.

## Scope
- In: `VenueAccount` (venue, platform, handle, source: detected | admin | prospect), `VenueAccountSnapshot` (account, month, followers, posts, avgViews, avgLikes, postsLast30Days), detection from the month's own-account posts, a `SCRAPE_OWN_ACCOUNTS` step, `ReportData.ownAccounts`, the report's "From your own account" box gains followers and growth, admin edit of handles on the venue page.
- Out: the account's post list beyond what `SCRAPE_VENUE_SOCIAL` already saves, story and DM data.
