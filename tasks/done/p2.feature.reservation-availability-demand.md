# Sample reservation availability as a demand signal

## Goal
For report venues on Quandoo, TheFork or OpenTable (from Google's `reserveTableUrl`), the pipeline samples twice a week how many slots are open for two people on the coming Friday and Saturday evening, two days ahead; the report and the portal show the "bookability" index over the month next to the views, the same way busyness is shown.

## Why
A fully booked Friday after a viral week is the proof the owner wants; availability is the only demand signal for sit-down places that do not show popular times well. Quandoo is the Berlin platform; TheFork covers much of Europe, which matters for expansion.

## Scope
- In: `VenueReservationSample` (venue, platform, `sampledAt`, `forDate`, `slotsOpen`, `slotsAsked`), a cron sharing the busyness schedule, the actors behind `ApifyService`, an index in `ReportData.demand` and a line in the busyness chart.
- Out: making bookings, prices of set menus.

## Pointers
- Actors: https://apify.com/stealth_mode/quandoo-restaurants-search-scraper, https://apify.com/clearpath/thefork-scraper, https://apify.com/canadesk/opentable.
- `busyness-index-from-popular-times` task (chart and cron pattern), `google-places-rich-fields` (`reserveTableUrl`).
