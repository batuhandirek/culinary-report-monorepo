# Leave a neighbour's own-account posts out of the report's "Who got talked about nearby"

## Goal
Each place's neighbour list counts posts by others only, like every other post count, so a venue's figure as a neighbour matches its own report.

## Why
`ReportService.neighbours` counts every matched mention of the district's venues, own-account posts included (no `ownPostSql`). The rule in `domain-venues-and-reports` is that every count of posts by others leaves out the brand's own account. On dev September 2026, DREI. (own account @drei.berlin, 5 own posts) shows 13 posts / 742.2K in Nhas City Chicken's Sonnenallee place section and 8 / 673.6K in its own report, and the place's own row (posts by others) is ranked against neighbours inflated by self-promotion.

## Scope
- In: `ReportService.neighbours` leaves out posts whose account is the neighbour's brand's (`ownPostSql('p', 'v.brand_id')`); check `sum(p.views)` counts a post once per venue; a PGlite case; rebuild September's reports (free besides two LLM calls each).
- Out: the neighbourhood openings line (`reports/util/neighbourhood.ts`, another task's file).

## Pointers
- `apps/backend/src/modules/reports/services/report.service.ts` (`neighbours`)
- `apps/backend/src/modules/social/util/accounts.ts` (`ownPostSql`)
- Tests: `reports/tests/report-brand.pglite.spec.ts` or `report.service.pglite.spec.ts`

## Acceptance
- [x] PGlite: a neighbour's own-account post is not counted in its posts or views. (`reports/tests/report.service.pglite.spec.ts`, "counts a neighbour's posts by others, each once, as its own report does": also a post naming the neighbour twice counts once in posts and views)
- [x] After a rebuild, DREI.'s figures as a neighbour match its own report's posts by others for the month. (8 posts / 673,571 views in Nhas City Chicken's and Die Zimtschnecke's reports and in its own place row, rebuilt 12:13–12:18 UTC; 13 / 742,226 before)
