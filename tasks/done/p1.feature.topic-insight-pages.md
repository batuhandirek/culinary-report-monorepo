# Make the topics admin an insight page, with a page per topic

## Goal
`/admin/topics` ranks the city's topics by what is happening: venues tagged (supply), posts by others this month and the change (buzz), Google searches now and the change with a 12-month line (demand). Each row opens `/admin/topics/[key]`: where the topic sits (parent, children), its search line and related searches, its mentions over twelve months, its venues (primary first, with the month's posts) and its children's figures. Unrecognised words move to their own tab with example venues.

## Why
The first page listed the taxonomy (aliases, ids) but answered no question an admin has: what is growing, where is demand without supply, which venues are this food. The unmapped list showed words without context, so nobody could act on them.

## Scope
- In: `TopicInsightService` (trends module) `list(cityId, month)` and `detail(cityId, key, month)`, a pure mention counter shared with `SearchInterestService`, `GET /admin/topic-insights` and `/admin/topic-insights/:key`, example venues for unrecognised words, the two pages (editing through the existing dialog).
- Out: cross-topic search levels (`public-food-trends-report`), the four remaining `normalizeCuisine` readers (`cuisine-consumers-onto-topics`).

## Pointers
- `trends/services/search-interest.service.ts` (`topicShares`), `food-topics/services/venue-food-topic.service.ts` (`tags`), `food-topics/services/food-topic.service.ts` (`overview`)
