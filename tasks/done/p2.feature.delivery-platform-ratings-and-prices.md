# Pull delivery ratings and menu prices from Lieferando and Wolt for price positioning

## Goal
For report venues listed on Lieferando or Wolt (known from Google's `orderBy` links or a name search), the pipeline stores the platform rating and review count, delivery fee and minimum order, and the menu with prices; the report gets "Your prices next to your competitors'" (median main course, the venue's signature dishes against the same dish nearby) and a delivery-reputation line, and the price complaint share is read against the actual price position.

## Why
"Expensive" in reviews means nothing without the prices; with the menu, the report can say "your döner is €1.50 above the Kreuzberg median and price is criticised in 18 % of your reviews, 6 % at the median". Delivery ratings are a second reputation the owner rarely watches. Wolt is $0.80 per 1,000 items; Lieferando menus are priced per row and can reach $25 for a hundred restaurants, so scrape only report venues and their competitors.

## Scope
- In: `VenueDeliveryListing` (venue, platform, url, rating, reviewsCount, deliveryFee, minOrder, `fetchedAt`), `MenuItem` (listing, name, price, category, month), a `SCRAPE_DELIVERY` step with its budget, dish-name matching to the report's dishes with `dishKey`, `ReportData.prices` and the block.
- Out: Uber Eats (German coverage unconfirmed), order volumes (not public).
