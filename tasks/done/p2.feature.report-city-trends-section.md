# Put the city's trends and a city baseline into each report

## Goal
A report section "What Berlin is talking about" shows the month's rising cuisines and dishes and where the venue stands ("ramen mentions doubled; you were one of nine ramen places mentioned, fourth by views"), plus a baseline row: the venue's views, stars this month and reply rate against the median of the month's report venues. The highlights model gets both.

## Why
Owners ask "is it just me, or is everyone quiet this month". A city baseline answers it, and the trend turns the report from a mirror into market intelligence. The queries come from `city-trends-page`; this task is the `ReportData` fields, the section and the prompt.

## Scope
- In: `ReportData.city` (top five rising cuisines and dishes with share deltas; the venue's cuisine, its rank among that cuisine's venues by views; medians of views, posts, avg stars and reply rate across the month's report venues, with the venue's percentile), the section in `ReportView` (hidden when the month has fewer than 200 matched mentions), prompt input and one sentence of instruction.
- Out: a public trend report.

## Pointers
- `apps/backend/src/modules/reports/services/report.service.ts` (`collect`, `highlightsInput`), `interfaces/report-data.interface.ts`.
- The `TrendService` from `city-trends-page`.
- `apps/frontend/src/views/report/ReportView.svelte` (`sections` numbering).
