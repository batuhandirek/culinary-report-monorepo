# Draft replies to the month's unanswered reviews

## Goal
For each unanswered written review of the month (critical ones first, up to ten) the report carries a ready-to-post reply in the review's language, in the venue's voice (short, specific, no excuses), under "Replies ready to post". The admin can regenerate them.

## Why
Low reply rate is the most common nameable problem in the data and the easiest to fix; a report that says "you answered 1 %" is a scolding, one that hands over ten replies is a service. It is the pitch for venues like Nhas City Chicken.

## Scope
- In: `ReviewReplyAgent` (input: venue name, category, the review text and stars, the month's praised topics; output: reply ≤ 80 words, language of the review), `ReportData.reviews.replyDrafts` (reviewId, draft) filled at build for unanswered written reviews, critical first, cap ten; the report section with the review excerpt and the draft; the appendix marks reviews that have a draft.
- Out: posting the replies (Google Business Profile API needs the owner's account), a per-venue voice setting (a later task).

## Pointers
- `apps/backend/src/modules/reports/agents/report-highlights.agent.ts` (agent pattern), `services/report.service.ts`.
- `apps/backend/src/modules/venues/entities/google-review.entity.ts` (`ownerResponse`, `language`).
- `apps/frontend/src/views/report/ReportView.svelte`.
