# Make post authors accounts and creators, and match venues' own accounts by their handles

## Goal
Every post's author is a `SocialAccount` (one handle on one platform), every account belongs to a `Creator` (the person behind one or more accounts), and an account that is a brand's own is linked to the brand once, by a matcher an admin can correct. Everything that asks "is this post by the venue itself?" reads that link. The admin has a page of the venues' own accounts with their figures, one for creators, and a list of likely matches to confirm.

## Why
- Own accounts were recognised by name on every read (`isOwnAccount` against `ownNames`), loosely: city-wide, 774 of 2,518 handles "matched" some venue, many wrongly (`@foodgrrrl` → "FOOD | Gnocchi Affair", `@crossfatberlin` → "Coffee Cross"). An admin could not correct it, and removing a `BrandAccount` did not change what reports counted as own.
- Detection only ran for the month's report brands, from posts already matched to them: on 2026-10-19 dev had 4 own accounts, while handles like `@soriramen.berlin`, `@markthalleneun`, `@misspho.berlin` were never linked.
- 192 venues have an Instagram profile as their Google website: a certain link nobody read.
- 25 handles exist on both TikTok and Instagram with the same letters (`polina_iz_berlina` / `polina.iz.berlina`): one person, counted twice in creator tables.

## Design
- `SocialAccount` (social module): `platform` + `handle` unique, `displayName`, latest `followers`, `city` (first seen), `creator` (required; a subscriber gives a new account a creator of its own), the brand link (`brand`, `branch`, `brandSource` handle | website | prospect | admin), the matcher's open suggestion (`suggestedBrand`, `suggestedScore`) and `rejectedBrandIds` (an admin's "not theirs", final). `SocialPost.account` links the post; `author` stays as its handle for search and display.
- `Creator`: `name`, its accounts. A new account joins the creator holding the same compact handle on the other platform; an admin merges and splits.
- `SocialAccountSnapshot` replaces `BrandAccountSnapshot` (account × month); `BrandAccount` is gone.
- Matching (`AccountMatchingService`, pure scorer `social/util/account-match.ts`), free, after each `MATCH_MENTIONS` and from the admin page: a branch's website or the prospect's handle is certain; a handle that is the brand's (or a branch's) name once city words and generic affixes are stripped is linked when one brand in the city has it; a looser match is linked only with evidence (the account posted about the brand or tagged its place), else suggested.

## Acceptance
- [x] PGlite: accounts and creators created on post save; cross-platform grouping; the scorer's tiers on real handles from dev; links, suggestions, rejections final; website and prospect links (`social/tests/account-match.spec.ts`, `account-matching.pglite.spec.ts`, `own-account.pglite.spec.ts`).
- [x] Reports, brand list and summary, top brands, lead score, trends, comments and portal posts read "own" from the link (`ownPostSql` / `post.account.brand`); `isOwnAccount` and `ownNames` are gone.
- [x] One Social page (`/admin/posts`, at the user's request instead of separate pages): tabs Posts, Creators, Own accounts, To review; a creator's page at `/admin/creators/[id]`; own accounts with figures on the brand Overview and the branch's Place tab.
