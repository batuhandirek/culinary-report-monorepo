# One `instagram-hashtags` atom for every Instagram hashtag scrape

## Goal
Every `apify/instagram-scraper` run on tag URLs goes through one atom, `pipeline/atoms/instagram-hashtags/` (`instagram-hashtags.run`), built like `pipeline/atoms/places/`: a caller gives the hashtags, whether it wants posts or reels, the day they start and the results per tag; the atom builds the input in a step, runs the actor as a child on the `apify` queue with the operation as owner, and saves the posts and their finds through `SocialScrapeService`'s one save in a step. The same actor's post-counts mode (`directUrls`, the daily refresh) is a second search mode of the atom or its own, decided when built.

## Why
`scrape-instagram` (month: posts and reels in two runs; week: reels; day: the newest page of posts) runs the actor inline inside its steps through `SocialScrapeService.scrapeCityInstagram` and its recent variants; the post-counts refresh runs it inline too. One atom puts them on the `apify` queue and makes the month's two runs two visible children.

## Scope
- In: the atom; `SocialScrapeService` split into plan (the tags, the share of the budget per run, the per-tag limits) and save for Instagram; the three `scrape-instagram` workflows composed plan → `instagram-hashtags.call` (the month's two runs started then awaited in order) → save; the suites; the skills and `operations.md`.
- Out: the location actor (venue place pages), unless it fits as a mode.
