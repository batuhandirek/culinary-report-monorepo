# Re-read dev's July–September posts and October's tail for the "just opened" flag

## Goal
Every stored post inside the lead score's six-month window has been read for `isNewOpening`, so a place a creator said had just opened in July–September counts as opened in the lead score, the "New venues" filter and the Trends openings panel.

## Why
The flag shipped on 2026-10-22; mentions extracted before carry `false`. October's 1,100 most viewed of 1,306 such posts were re-read; the 206 least viewed, and July (95), August (101) and September (466) posts with mentions, were not. `LEAD_NEW_PLACE_MONTHS` is 6, so those months still feed the bonus.

## Scope
- In: `POST /admin/mentions/reflag-openings` (admin session; `INTEGRATION_ADMIN_EMAIL` / `INTEGRATION_ADMIN_PASSWORD` from `env/server/.env.local`, `POST /api/auth/login/email`) with `month`, Berlin's `cityId`, `analysedBefore: "2026-10-22T00:00:00.000Z"`, `limit: 100`, paging by `nextOffset` until `remaining` is 0: October from `offset: 1100`, then 2026-09, 2026-08, 2026-07. About 870 posts at ~3,000 tokens (~2.6M on `gpt-5.4-mini`), inside one account's daily allowance; stop on credit or rate-limit errors. Then `SCORE_LEADS` for Berlin's report month from the Data page so the bonus shows.
- Out: changing the flag's wording (see `new-branch-as-opening`), re-extracting anything else.

## Pointers
- `apps/backend/src/modules/social/services/mention-extraction.service.ts` (`reflagOpenings`), `controllers/admin.opening-flags.controller.ts`
