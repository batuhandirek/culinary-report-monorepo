# Give the portal a page for the computed patterns across months

## Goal
`/portal/[venueId]/patterns` shows the relations `ReportData.correlations` holds, month by month from the stored reports (buzz to reviews, complaint times, paid against organic), and busyness next to the views by week across months, not just inside one report.

## Why
Each report shows its month's relations; an owner asking "do our creator weeks fill the place?" wants them across months. The portal's report tab already renders the block, so this is the cross-month view.

## Scope
- In: a series endpoint reading `correlations` / `busyness` from stored reports (like `util/portal-series.ts`), a link tab, charts per the Trends tab rules (one measure per chart, table under each).
- Out: recomputing relations at request time.

## Pointers
- `apps/backend/src/modules/reports/util/correlations.ts`, `reports/services/report-signals.service.ts`, `portal/util/portal-series.ts`.
- `apps/frontend/src/views/report/ReportPatterns.svelte`, `routes/(portal)/portal/(app)/[venueId]/trends/components/`.

## Acceptance
- [x] PGlite: the endpoint answers the months' relations, absent where a report lacks them, behind `VenueAccessGuard`.
- [ ] The page shows them for a venue with two rebuilt reports. — Partly: no dev report holds `correlations` (Döner Pro, 10 posts / 200 reviews, has reviews for 2026-09 only, so no baseline; the others are below the floors), and rebuilding is the report-content agent's job. Checked in the running app instead: the weeks section on Döner Pro's real data, and the relations section with two months' relations injected into the response in the browser (no data written).

None.
