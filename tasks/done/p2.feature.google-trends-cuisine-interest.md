# Add Google search interest per cuisine to the city trends

## Goal
The trends page shows, per cuisine, Google search interest in the city's region over the last twelve months next to the share of social mentions, and the delta of both; the report's trend section can say "ramen: mentions up 2×, searches up 40 %". Venue-name interest is stored when Google has enough data.

## Why
Social buzz leads, search demand follows; a cuisine that rises on TikTok but not in search is early, one that rises in both is a trend. Trends data is almost free and is the one source that does not depend on our scrape budget.

## Scope
- In: `TrendSearchInterest` (city, term, month, interest 0–100), a monthly step pulling the city's cuisine terms (from the mentions' cuisines, top 30) and the WON prospects' venue names with the region set to the city's country and the city where Trends supports it, the chart and the delta on the trends page, `ReportData.city` gains the search deltas.
- Out: keyword research beyond cuisines and venue names.

## Pointers
- Actor: https://apify.com/apify/google-trends-scraper.
- `city-trends-page` task (`TrendService`), `multi-city-config` (country and region per city).

## Acceptance
- [x] PGlite: interest rows per term and month; terms with no data stored as null, not zero. (`trends/tests/search-interest.pglite.spec.ts`, `pipeline/tests/pipeline-search-interest.pglite.spec.ts`)
- [ ] The trends page shows the search line next to the mention share. Built (`SearchInterestPanel` on `/admin/trends`) and svelte-checked, but dev has no pulled data (no Apify run allowed) and the dev stack was down, so it is unseen.
