# Annotate the month with weather and city events

## Goal
Each city stores daily weather (max temperature, rain) from Open-Meteo and a list of events and holidays (public holidays from a free API, school holidays and major events entered by the admin on the city page); the portal charts show them as bands, and the report's quiet or loud weeks get a context line ("rain on three of four weekends; the marathon weekend").

## Why
A bad month with a reason is not a bad month. Outdoor-seating venues (`additionalInfo` from `google-places-rich-fields`) live and die by the weather, and holiday weeks move everything. Both sources are free.

## Scope
- In: `CityDay` (city, date, tMax, rainMm, holiday name or null, events jsonb), a daily cron filling yesterday for active cities, admin editing of events on the city page, `ReportData.context` (weekend rain count, holidays, events), chart bands in the portal.
- Out: forecasts, paid event feeds.

## Pointers
- Open-Meteo archive API (free, no key), Nager.Date public holidays API; `multi-city-config` task (city page, timezone, country).

## Acceptance
- [x] PGlite: a day is stored once; the report context counts rainy weekends. (`cities/tests/city-day.pglite.spec.ts`)

## Log
- 2026-10-17: Events are their own rows, not `CityDay.events` jsonb: `CityEvent` (city, kind `school_holiday` | `event`, name, startDate, endDate, both days included). A school holiday spans weeks, and editing or removing one range is one row instead of fifteen. `CityDay` (city, date text, tMax, rainMm, holiday) is unique per city and date; a later fetch updates it (the archive revises its newest days) and an empty newest day keeps the earlier figures.
- 2026-10-17: Sources (free, no key) behind `CityContextSourcesService`: Open-Meteo's archive API (`temperature_2m_max`, `precipitation_sum`, in the city's time zone; it has data up to yesterday) and Nager.Date `PublicHolidays/{year}/{country}`, kept when `global` or `counties` holds the city's new `City.region` (ISO 3166-2, e.g. DE-BE). English holiday names, since reports are English. Weather needs `City.lat/lng`.
- 2026-10-17: `CityDayService` is the entry point (`fill(from, to)`, `fillRecent`, `days`, `month`, `months`, event CRUD); `CityDayCron` (`city-days`, 05:00 UTC) refetches the last 7 local days up to yesterday for active cities. Admin: `/admin/cities/:id/days`, `/days/fill`, `/context?month=`, `/events` (+ PATCH/DELETE), and a Calendar tab on the city page (month summary, Fetch, events editor, the days).
- 2026-10-17: Month context (`util/month-context.ts`, pure): weekends = Saturdays in the month; a weekend is rainy when its Saturday or Sunday had ≥ 1 mm (the Sunday may be the next month's 1st); rain days ≥ 1 mm; mean high. Weather figures are left out when under 90 % of the month's days have weather, so a gap never reads as a dry month. `ReportData.context` (set in `ReportService.build`), the highlights prompt gets it as `cityCalendar` with a one-clause rule, and `ReportView` prints "The month in Berlin: Rain on 2 of 4 weekends · German Unity Day (3 Oct) · …" under the KPIs (`lib/city-calendar.ts`). Reports built before this have no line until rebuilt.
- 2026-10-17: Portal: `GET …/series` gains `calendar` (one `MonthContext` per month, from `CityDayService.months`, not from reports, so every month has it); the Trends tab shades holidays (narrow, darker) and events (wider, light) inside each month's band, names the active month's in the readout, and prints the picked month's line.
- 2026-10-17: Dev backfill: Berlin 2026-05-01 → 2026-10-16, 169 days (weather + 4 public holidays: Labour Day, Ascension, Whit Monday, German Unity Day), written as SQL from one Open-Meteo and one Nager.Date call since the dev backend was down. No school holidays or events entered: those are the admin's.
