# Suggest a merge when two brands' branches give the same Instagram or TikTok profile

## Goal
"Look like one brand" (`suggestBrands`) also offers venues of different brands whose Google website is the same Instagram or TikTok profile, so an unmerged chain's branches come up together and their own account is no longer split between them.

## Why
On dev Berlin (2026-10-19) the account matcher found five profiles listed as the website of two or three brands each: `croissantxcouture`, `kaffee_buro`, `kinza.berlin`, `loveatfirstbite_berlin`, `thenoodletown` — one business each, not merged. The first brand gets the account link (`AccountMatchingService.linkWebsites`); the others' reports then count the business's own posts as word of mouth. `suggestBrands` groups by a shared website but skips platform profiles (`faviconSite`), since a shared Lieferando or Linktree page says nothing; a shared Instagram profile does.

## Scope
- In: a profile website (`profileOf`) shared by venues of 2+ brands in a city becomes a suggestion (reason "same Instagram" / "same TikTok"), keyed like the others so a dismissal sticks.
- Out: merging automatically; profiles found any other way than the Google website.

## Pointers
- `apps/backend/src/modules/venues/util/brands.ts` — `suggestBrands` (the website group).
- `apps/backend/src/modules/social/util/accounts.ts` — `profileOf`.
- `apps/backend/src/modules/venues/tests/brands.spec.ts` — suggestion cases.
