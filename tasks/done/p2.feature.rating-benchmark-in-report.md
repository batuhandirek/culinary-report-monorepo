# Put the venue's Google rating against its district's and its kind of food's median in the report

## Goal
The report's cover says where the venue's all-time Google rating stands: "4.3 · above 70 % of Kreuzberg's 212 food venues · above 55 % of Berlin's 61 döner places", and the highlights prompt may quote it. The same figures feed the lead score's "nameable problem" (below the district median is a problem we can name without scraping a review) and the sales hook.

## Why
It is the comparison every owner understands first, it needs no scraping (the city list holds every venue's rating, review count, district and category; the primary food topic is worked out on read), and it is the one line a salesperson can say on a call to any of the 4,991 venues, not only the ~120 with reviews.

## Scope
- In: `ReportData.venue.ratingBenchmark` with `district` and `topic` entries (`median`, `percentile`, `venues`, the label), computed at build like the city baseline (`ReportBaselineMetric`: ties half, floors at 5 venues, open food venues with a rating and 20+ reviews so one-review places don't set the median); the cover line; a sentence for the highlights prompt; the lead score reading the district percentile as a problem signal when reviews are absent (weight unchanged).
- Out: a benchmark on this month's stars (that is the city section's baseline over report brands); a public benchmark page (the public content doc).
