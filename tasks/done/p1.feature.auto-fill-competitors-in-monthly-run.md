# Fill competitors for report brands that have none inside the monthly run, before the review scrape

## Goal
Every report built by the monthly cron has a "How you compare" section: a report brand with no brand-wide competitor gets its five best suggestions saved as competitors before `SCRAPE_REVIEWS` runs, so the competitors' reviews are scraped in the same run. Brands that already have any brand-wide competitor (admin's or owner's picks) are never touched.

## Why
`PipelineService.fillCompetitors` exists only behind `POST /admin/pipeline/fill-competitors` (the Data page). The cron queues the run at 06:00 UTC on the 2nd with nobody awake, so each month every brand new to the top 20 gets a report without the comparison, without competitor reviews, and without the competitor columns in weekend tables and prices. On dev, 17 of September's 21 reports have competitors, all filled by hand. The comparison is one of the three findings and the strongest pitch line ("wait was criticised in 23 % of your reviews and 8 % of Burgerme's").

Stability is kept: `CompetitorService.fill` only fills brands with **no** brand-wide competitor, so a list, once set, never changes on its own (the rule in the skill).

## Scope
- In: a free step `FILL_COMPETITORS` in `MONTHLY_STEPS` right before `SCRAPE_REVIEWS` (after the last `MATCH_MENTIONS`, so `topBrands` is final), calling `competitorService.fill` for the month's report brands; its result (`brands`, `filled`) in the run; the Data page's step list and `lib/pipeline-steps.ts`; "Finish the month" treats it as free (it has no budget).
- Out: changing suggestions' scoring; re-filling brands whose list was emptied by an owner (an empty list after a removal is their choice: keep `fill` as is, it only looks at the current rows); a separate cron.
