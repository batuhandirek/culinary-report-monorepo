# Make the brand the unit: every venue is a branch of a brand

## Goal
Every venue belongs to a brand (a one-branch brand by default). The business things are keyed by the brand: reports (brand × city × month), prospects, portal access, the lead score, the monthly top list, mentions. The things tied to an address stay on the venue. The pinned "main branch" goes away.

## Why
The customer is the business, not the address. With brands layered over venue-keyed storage, one branch had to stand in for the brand (`Venue.brandMain`), and every query had to map units back and forth (`coalesce(brand_id, id)`, `mainOf`, `withBranches`). Gaps that came from that: an owner got access to one branch, the portal's posts covered one branch, a mention naming only the brand had to sit on some branch. Nothing is released, so this is the cheapest moment.

## Design
- **Brand**: `name`, `key` (no longer unique: two unrelated "Mustafa's" are two brands), `ownership` (null = unknown: the lead score falls back to the namesake rule; an admin-grouped brand always has it), `leadScore` / `leadSignals`, `branchesSearchedAt`, `excludedVenueIds`. No `status`: a dismissed suggestion is a `BrandDismissal` row (key). `Venue.brand` is required; a new venue gets its own brand (`VenueService.saveGooglePlace`).
- **Grouping is merging**: confirming a suggestion merges the venues' brands into one (`BrandService.merge`): the kept brand is the one with a prospect, else with reports, else the biggest. The others' reports, prospect, members, competitors and mentions move over, then they are deleted. Two prospects → 409 (remove one first). Clashing reports (same city × month) keep the kept brand's.
- **Taking a branch off** gives it a new brand of its own; the history stays with the old brand. A brand can't be deleted, only split.
- **Mentions**: `Mention.brand` is set whenever matched; `Mention.venue` only when the branch is known. `brandLevel` is gone (brand set, venue null). Location reads (districts, neighbours) use the venue and leave brand-level mentions out.
- **Matching**: a candidate carries its brand and how many open branches it has in the city; a one-branch brand is a plain venue (branch known).
- **Reports**: `Report.brand` + `Report.city` + `month`, unique. Built from the brand's branches in the city; the address-bound sections (district, neighbours, search ranks, busyness, open tables, prices, rating trend) describe the brand's most-reviewed open branch in the city, worked out at build (not stored, not pinned): `ReportData.venue` is that branch.
- **Lead score** on the brand: one score over all its open branches (every city), rescored by the run of any city it has a branch in, against the buzz of the brands of that city; the deny-list is its cities'.
- **Prospect** one per brand. **Portal**: `BrandMember` (user × brand), routes `/portal/brands/:brandId/*`, guard `BrandAccessGuard`.
- **Competitors**: `BrandCompetitor` — a brand's competition is a venue or a whole brand (exactly one), for the whole brand or for one of its branches (`branch`). A report compares the ones in its city (a brand competitor: its branches in the city together; a branch's only in the branch's city, labelled). Suggestions stay venue-based (from the brand's branch in the city, or the given branch) and can be added as the venue or its brand.
- **Search interest** links the brand (`TrendSearchInterest.brand`, kind `brand`).
- **Pipeline**: `topBrands(city, month)` → each with its branches in the city. Steps that work on places (reviews, details, delivery, busyness, press, search ranks) take the branches; steps that work on names (venue social) take the brand.

## Scope
- In: the above, backend and admin/portal frontend, a data migration of the dev data (one brand per venue without one; reports, prospects, members, competitors, mentions moved).
- Out: own accounts (`VenueAccount`) stay on the venue for now (follow-up task); cross-city reports (`brand-report-across-cities`) stay a separate task, now simpler; per-branch busyness/search-rank sections in one report.
