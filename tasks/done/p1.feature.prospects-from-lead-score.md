# Feed the sales pipeline from the lead score, and always report on won prospects

## Goal
The prospects list shows each venue's lead score and sorts by it; a "Suggested" view lists the highest-scoring venues that are not prospects yet with one-click add; and a venue whose prospect is `WON` gets its posts scraped, its reviews scraped and its report built every month whatever its rank.

## Why
`modules/outreach` records the conversation (stages, touches, follow-ups) but the venues on it are added by hand, and `WON` means nothing to the pipeline: a subscriber with a quiet month falls out of the top 20 and gets no report, which is the one thing they pay for (off-platform; the platform takes no payment). The lead score (task `venue-lead-score`) is what should decide who gets added.

## Scope
- In:
  - `PipelineService.topVenues()` = venues of `WON` prospects ∪ the top N by buzz, used by `SCRAPE_VENUE_SOCIAL`, `SCRAPE_REVIEWS`, `BUILD_REPORTS`.
  - `GET /admin/outreach/prospects`: `leadScore` on each item (from the venue), `sort=leadScore:desc`.
  - `GET /admin/outreach/suggested?size=`: venues ordered by `leadScore` with no prospect row, with the signal breakdown; `POST /admin/outreach/prospects` from a row (the existing endpoint); a bulk "add top 20" action.
  - Frontend: the score column and sort on the prospects list, the Suggested tab, an "Add to prospects" button on `/admin/venues/[venueId]`.
- Out: the score itself (`venue-lead-score`), the hook text (`prospect-pitch-hook`), e-mailing reports (`report-email-delivery`).
