# Compute the correlations the data already allows: buzz to reviews, complaints by day and hour

## Goal
Each report (and the portal) shows three computed relations: (1) views by week against new Google reviews by week, with the lag and a plain-language read ("the two creator videos in week 2 were followed by 14 reviews in week 3, twice the usual"); (2) complaints by weekday and hour of the review (as a proxy for the visit), against the busyness histogram when present ("wait complaints come from Saturday evenings, when you are at 90 %"); (3) paid or gifted posts against organic ones on views and on the review bump that followed.

## Why
These are the "so what" an owner cannot get from any single number, and they use data the pipeline already stores (`postedAt`, `views`, `publishedAt`, `stars`, `complaints`, `promotional`). The busyness series sharpens (2) but is not required.

## Scope
- In: pure functions in `util/correlations.ts` (weekly buckets in the city's timezone, a simple lagged ratio against the venue's own six-month baseline, weekday × hour matrices), `ReportData.correlations`, the prompt gets the sentences, a block in `ReportView` and a page in the portal. Say "followed by", never "caused"; hide a relation when the counts are below the floors (10 reviews, 3 posts).
- Out: statistical tests; cross-venue models.

## Pointers
- `apps/backend/src/modules/reports/util/summarize-report.ts` (where the month's mentions and reviews are already in memory), `interfaces/report-data.interface.ts`, `busyness-index-from-popular-times` task.
