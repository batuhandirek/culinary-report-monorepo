# Track what is getting popular in the city: cuisines, dishes, venues, creators

## Goal
`/admin/trends` shows, for a month and against the month before: cuisines by share of mentions and of views, dishes by share of posts and reviews naming them, rising venues (new in the top 50 by posts from others, biggest climbers), districts by share, creators by reach (posts, views, venues covered). Every figure is a share of the month, with the delta in points.

## Why
The pipeline already extracts `cuisine` per mention and `dishes` per mention and review but only ever aggregates per venue. City-level trends are three things at once: a report section ("matcha mentions tripled; you are one of four matcha places"; task `report-city-trends-section`), lead generation (a rising venue is a warm prospect for the lead score), and outreach material ("Berlin food trends, September 2026"). Shares, not counts: the scrape budget changes how many posts come in, so absolute counts would show the budget, not the city.

## Scope
- In:
  - `TrendService` SQL over `mentions` × `social_posts` (matched, in-city, own-account posts excluded) and `google_reviews`, grouped by month: cuisine (normalised: lowercase, accents stripped, a small synonym map such as viet/vietnamese, türkisch/turkish), dish (`dishKey` from `summarize-report.ts`), district, author.
  - `GET /admin/trends?month=YYYY-MM` returning the tables with current share, previous share, delta, and sample venues per cuisine and dish.
  - The page: one panel per table, bar rows (dataviz skill), month select, a "rising venues" list linking to venues; a note that figures are shares of what the scrape found.
- Out: the report section (own task), public pages, LLM-written commentary, source yield per dollar.
