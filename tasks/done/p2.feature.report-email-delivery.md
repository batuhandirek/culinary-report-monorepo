# E-mail the monthly report to won prospects

## Goal
After `BUILD_REPORTS`, every venue whose prospect is `WON` and has a `contactEmail` gets a mail in the prospect's `language` with the cover title, the headline, the three findings and the share link (PDF attached optionally), queued through `EmailService`; the send is logged as an `OutreachTouch` and shown on the report page.

## Why
Delivery is manual today (download the PDF, write a mail). Once a venue has said yes the report has to arrive by itself on the 2nd or 3rd of the month, or the subscription is a chore the user forgets.

## Scope
- In: `ReportReadyEmail` template in `packages/emails` (en and de), a `report-built` persisted event published by `ReportService.build` and a worker that mails `WON` prospects with an address, `Report.sentAt`, a "Send now" action on the admin report page, the touch log entry (kind EMAIL, template `report-ready`). No preference switch applies: the recipient is a business contact with no user account; the mail says to reply to stop, which sets `OPTED_OUT`.
- Out: a customer login, unsubscribe pages, mailing sample reports to prospects that are not won (that is outreach, done by hand from the prospect page).

## Pointers
- `packages/emails/src/emails/*.tsx`, `apps/backend/src/modules/email/services/email.service.ts`, `util/render-email.ts`.

## Acceptance
- [x] PGlite with the mail queue mocked: a `WON` prospect with an address gets one mail per build, a `CONTACTED` one none, a `WON` one without an address none; the touch is logged.
