# The owner portal: the latest report, the archive, the PDF, in their language

## Goal
`/portal` shows the owner's venue (a switcher when they have several), the latest report rendered with `ReportView`, an archive of every month with PDF download, and a settings page (contact e-mail, language). Phone-friendly, `noindex`, no admin chrome.

## Why
The report arrives by mail once a month and is gone; the portal is where it lives, where last month is one tap away, and where the charts (task `portal-trends-charts`) and explorers go. It is also what makes "subscribed" feel like a product rather than a PDF.

## Scope
- In: the `(portal)` route group with its own layout (logo, venue switcher, tabs: Report, Months, Trends, Reviews, Posts, Settings; the last four are filled by their own tasks), `GET /portal/venues` (member venues), `GET /portal/venues/:id/reports` (list) and `/reports/:reportId` (+ `/pdf`), reusing the report DTO without admin-only fields; the report page; the months list; settings (reads and writes the prospect's `language` and `contactEmail` through `OutreachService.update`); page titles and `noindex`.
- Out: charts, explorers, competitor picking (own tasks), a public marketing page.

## Pointers
- `apps/frontend/src/views/report/ReportView.svelte`, `routes/(admin)/+layout.svelte` (layout pattern to mirror, not reuse), `constants/ADMIN_MENUS.ts` (menu pattern).
