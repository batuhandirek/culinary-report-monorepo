# Rank the month's top venues by posts from others, not the venue's own

## Goal
`MentionMatchingService.topVenueIds` ignores mentions whose post author is the venue's own account, so a venue posting thirty times about itself does not take a report slot from a venue thirty creators talked about.

## Why
The monthly run spends the reviews budget and builds reports for the "most talked-about" venues, and the ranking SQL counts every mention. `summarizeReport` already keeps own-account posts out of the report; the selection disagrees with the report. `isOwnAccount` is a TypeScript function, so rank the top 3N in SQL with the author handle, filter in TypeScript, cut to N.

## Scope
- In: the ranking, the test, the skill sentence.
- Out: storing a venue's handles (the lead score task derives them the same way).
