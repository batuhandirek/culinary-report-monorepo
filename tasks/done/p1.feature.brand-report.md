# Build one monthly report per brand with a branch comparison, and sell it as one prospect

## Goal
A confirmed brand (from `venue-brands`) gets one monthly report: the brand's social buzz as a whole (branch and brand-level mentions), plus a branch-by-branch comparison of reviews, ratings, complaints and reply rate. The brand is one prospect in outreach, and its owner sees all branches in the portal.

## Why
The branch comparison is the pitch a single venue can't get ("your Mitte branch's reviews below 5★ are about waiting times; Prenzlauer Berg's aren't"). Without it, a chain owner gets three thin reports, and the mentions on one branch only show the brand's buzz under one branch. Starts after `venue-brands`.

## Scope
- In:
  - **Report scope** per brand: `Brand.reportScope` `city` (default) or `all`. With `city`, a brand in Berlin and Hamburg gets one report per city, each from that city's branches and posts. With `all`, one report covers every covered city: buzz summed across cities, the branch comparison grouped by city. Trends and the city section stay per city, since each city's posts come from its own sources and budget. An `all` report waits until every city's monthly run has finished (the cron runs cities one after another).
  - `PipelineService.topVenues` already lists a brand once, by its main branch (`topVenueIds`, from `venue-brands`); won prospects must too. A brand's steps (`SCRAPE_REVIEWS`, `REFRESH_VENUE_DETAILS`, own accounts, press, …) cover every branch inside the same budget.
  - `ReportData` for a brand: today's sections from the brand's combined posts, a `branches` section (per branch: rating, rating trend, written reviews, top praise and complaints, reply rate, the 5★-vs-rest split where each side has 3+ written reviews), and the prompt told to compare branches only on percentages and with the existing 5-review floor.
  - `Report` keyed by brand × month (or by its main branch; decide). The portal's series and patterns read it.
  - Outreach: `Prospect` per brand (today `OneToOne` with `Venue` in `outreach/entities/prospect.entity.ts`). A won brand gets every branch's review watch and alerts.
  - Portal: a membership on a brand opens its report and each branch's reviews.
- Out: per-branch pricing of the product.

## Pointers
- How brands are stored (`venue-brands`, in the `domain-venues-and-reports` skill): `Venue.brand`; a mention naming only the brand is linked to the main branch with `Mention.brandLevel`, so a brand's buzz is every mention on its branches, and a branch's own buzz is its mentions without `brandLevel`.
