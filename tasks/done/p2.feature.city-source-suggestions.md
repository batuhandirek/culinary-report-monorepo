# Suggest hashtags, searches and districts for a new city

## Goal
On a new city's page, "Suggest sources" asks an LLM for twenty TikTok hashtags, twenty searches and ten Instagram hashtags in the city's language and English (the way `BERLIN_HASHTAGS` was built by hand), and "Suggest districts" fills the district list with postal codes from a first Google Maps scrape of the city; the admin reviews and enables them.

## Why
Opening a city is otherwise a day of guessing hashtags. The Berlin lists were ranked by yield over months; a new city needs a decent first list, then the yield view (in `multi-city-config`) prunes it.

## Scope
- In: `CitySourceSuggestionAgent` (input: city, country, language, cuisine notes; output: the three lists with a one-line reason each), `POST /admin/cities/:id/suggest-sources` storing suggestions as disabled `CitySource` rows marked `suggested`, districts from the postal codes seen in the venue scrape (majority district per postal code, the `fillDistricts` idea in reverse), the UI buttons and a review list.
- Out: auto-enabling anything.

## Pointers
- `multi-city-config` task, `apps/backend/src/modules/reports/agents/report-highlights.agent.ts` (agent pattern), `venue.service.ts` (`fillDistricts`).

## Acceptance
- [x] A new city gets suggested sources as disabled rows the admin can enable; districts fill after a venue scrape. (PGlite: `cities/tests/city-suggestion.pglite.spec.ts`; one real agent call for a München test input; the UI is svelte-checked but not clicked through: the dev stack was down.)
