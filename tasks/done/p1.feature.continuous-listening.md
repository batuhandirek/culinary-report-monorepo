# Listen continuously (weekly TikTok scrapes) so a month is covered when its report is built

## Goal
By the 2nd of a month, the month before has most of its TikTok posts stored, because the city's sources were scraped every week of it — not once, on the 2nd, when a hashtag feed mostly returns other months.

## Why
- `clockworks/tiktok-scraper` hashtag feeds are not newest-first and ignore `oldestPostDateUnified` / `newestPostDate` (tested 2026-10-18: 20 `#berlinfood` results from Nov 2025 to Oct 2026, 3 in September). The monthly `SCRAPE_TIKTOK` pays for ~2,000 hashtag items and keeps the ones after the month's start; on 2026-10-18 a September run found 3,409 items and saved 1,102, most of them October's.
- The search (`novi/advanced-search-tiktok-api`, `publishTime: THREE_MONTH`, by relevance) is the month-bounded part, and it only covers 30–100 results a query.
- Instagram already works this way (`SCRAPE_INSTAGRAM_RECENT`, daily), for the same reason (one page a hashtag on Free).
- The Data page shows the result: a month's TikTok count depends on when it was scraped, so months are not comparable.

## Scope
- In: a weekly cron (`SCRAPE_TIKTOK_RECENT`: the searches with `publishTime: ONE_WEEK` if the actor has it, else `ONE_MONTH`, and the hashtags at a smaller `resultsPerPage`), extraction and matching after it; `MONTHLY` keeps a smaller month-end search. Budgets so the month costs about what `SCRAPE_TIKTOK` costs today ($15).
- Out: changing actors (separate research if the weekly yield is poor).

## Pointers
- `social/services/social-scrape.service.ts` (`scrapeCityTikTok`, `searchTikTok`), `pipeline/crons/instagram-recent.cron.ts` (the pattern), `pipeline/constants/pipeline.constant.ts`
- Data page (`overview/services/data-coverage.service.ts`) to watch the weekly fill

## Acceptance
- [x] PGlite: the weekly cron queues one run per cron city with TikTok sources; its search input asks for the last week (`pipeline/tests/weekly-listening.pglite.spec.ts`: TikTok + reels runs, then extraction and matching, per cron city; `publishTime: 'WEEK'`, 30 a query, hashtag pages sized to the budget; the month-end pass; reels; the Data page rows).
- [ ] After a month of weekly runs, the Data page's TikTok count for the month is within 20 % of the count a month-end search would add (measured once).

## Open questions
- Weekly or twice a week? Assumption: weekly (Sundays), cheap enough to try.
