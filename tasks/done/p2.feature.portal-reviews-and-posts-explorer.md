# Portal explorers: every review and every post, filterable, with reply drafts

## Goal
The portal's Reviews tab lists the venue's Google reviews with filters (month, stars, topic, answered or not, language) and shows the reply draft under an unanswered one with a copy button; the Posts tab lists every post about the venue (creator, followers, platform, views, paid flag, summary, link) with month and platform filters and a "creators" grouping.

## Why
The report shows six posts and six critical reviews; the owner wants the rest, and "unanswered, with a draft" is the daily to-do the reply rate finding points at.

## Scope
- In: `GET /portal/venues/:id/reviews` and `/posts` (paginated, filters as query params, no reviewer names), the two tabs with `PaginatedDataTable`-like lists adapted to the portal look, the copy-draft control, an "answered" toggle the owner can set by hand (`GoogleReview.markedRepliedAt`) until the next scrape confirms it.
- Out: posting replies, comment threads.

## Pointers
- `apps/backend/src/modules/venues/controllers/admin.venues.controller.ts` (`listVenueReviews`), `modules/social/controllers/admin.social.controller.ts` (posts list), `modules/reports/interfaces/report-data.interface.ts` (`replyDrafts`).
- `apps/frontend/src/ui/components/paginated-data-table/`, `SlideFetcher.svelte`.
