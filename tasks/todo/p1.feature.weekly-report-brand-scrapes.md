# Scrape the report brands' comments, press and name searches weekly, and shrink MONTHLY to closing the month

## Goal
A report brand's top posts' comments, its press, its name searches and its TripAdvisor reviews
come in every week of the month instead of once on the 2nd, at about the same monthly cost; the
monthly run only closes the month (final brands, competitors, the review backfill and catch-up,
details, delivery, search ranks, Trends, reports, scores). Google reviews are done: every open
venue's come in weekly with `SNAPSHOT_RATINGS` since 2026-11-04 (per-venue stretch
`reviewsFetchedFrom`..`reviewsFetchedAt`; `SCRAPE_REVIEWS` starts at the last fetch).

## Why
On the cloned production DB (2026-11-04) review text exists only from the monthly scrape on the
2nd (`SCRAPE_REVIEWS`, ~136 venues) plus the weekly watch for won prospects (none yet), so the
admin sees no review between the 2nd of two months. Reading is continuous since
`decouple-reading-and-track-accounts`, so weekly scrapes would be read within the hour.

## Scope
- In:
  - TripAdvisor reviews weekly for the report brands (Google's are weekly for every venue already).
  - `WATCH_REVIEWS`: its scrape is now mostly what the Tuesday fetch has; it could alert from the
    stored rows alone.
  - Comments: a post's comments when it turns 7 days old (with its count read), not on the 2nd.
  - Press weekly over the last 8 days; venue social (name searches) weekly.
  - `MONTHLY_STEPS` shrinks (a re-keying migration, as `Migration20261104150000_monthly_without_reading`).
- Out: the choice of report brands itself; Instagram comments.

- [ ] The month's Apify spend on these steps measured before and after (`apify_runs`), within ~20 %.
