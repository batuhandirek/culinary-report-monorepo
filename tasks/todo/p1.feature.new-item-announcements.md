# Know when a place announces a new dish, drink or menu

## Goal
A post that announces something new on a place's menu ("Meet our new Mint & Ginger Hot Drink!") is stored as a claim
saying so, about that place, with the item's wording as a term, so we can list per city and month what is new on
menus, who announced it (the brand's own account or a creator), and the topics it belongs to.

## Why
New dishes are among the strongest food-culture signals (what launches, which trends spread from place to place)
and the brand's own announcements are the cheapest source of them. Today the example post
(`/admin/posts/94f59903-f61a-4b29-9dd8-19675afc0bf1`, @chensbeefnoodlehouse, 2026-10-28) holds one migrated
dish-only claim, "Mint & Ginger Hot Drink", with no text, no flag and no aspect: nothing says it is new.

## Scope
- In: agent A (`PostClaimsAgent`, and `ReviewClaimsAgent` for "they have a new …") writing an announcement as a claim
  ("Chen's has a new Mint & Ginger hot drink") flagged `change`, the item as a food wording; an aspect for it
  (assumption: a new leaf under `food`, e.g. `menu_change`: "a dish, drink or menu added, or taken off", by the admin
  topic dialog or a seed migration, since aspects are rows); a reader "new on menus" (city × month: the item, its
  topics, the place, the source, own post or not) on the Trends page or the Claims tab as a filter; tests.
- Out: delivery menus (`MenuItem`) diffs month to month (a separate, free signal worth its own task), reports and the
  front page using it (later), anything beyond what agent A already reads.

## Pointers
- `apps/backend/src/modules/social/agents/post-claims.agent.ts` (A on posts: the change wording)
- `apps/backend/src/modules/statements/agents/claim-parts.agent.ts` (B: aspect and change)
- `apps/backend/src/modules/topics/constants/aspect-seed.constant.ts` (the aspects as seeded)
- `apps/backend/src/modules/statements/services/statement.service.ts` (`list`: the Claims tab's filters)
- Own posts are decided at read time (`ownPostSql`); an announcement by the brand itself is wanted here, unlike word of mouth.
