# Decide what makes a post promotional

## Goal
`SocialPost.isPromotional` means one thing, written in `PostClaimsAgent`'s rules, and the references follow it.

## Why
Nothing defines it today beyond the schema hint "Paid partnership, ad, gifted meal, invited by, or collab
signals". Two strong readers (Opus, gpt-5.4) given the same rules agree on it in only 69% of held-back posts.
The misses on the train side (31 of 141):
- 20 ads missed, 19 of them a venue advertising itself in its own voice ("unser Tipp", "kommt vorbei", its
  address and phone); 8 of these come from the venue's own linked account.
- 11 organic posts flagged: 3 are creator disclaimers saying the post is *not* paid ("unbezahlte Werbung",
  "[unpaid advertisement]"), 4 were long articles where one part's wording flagged the whole article (fixed:
  the first pass decides now), 2 are creators plugging their own guide or app.

## Options
1. **Paid or invited only.** True when someone with a stake paid or invited the creator: a paid partnership,
   a gifted meal, an invitation, a collab, a giveaway. A venue's own post is not "promotional"; it is an own
   post (`ownPostSql`) anyway.
2. **Anything selling a venue on behalf of someone with a stake** (recommended by the analyst): option 1, plus a
   venue, food truck or brand advertising itself in its own voice ("our new menu", "come by", its address as
   a call to visit). False for a creator's own unpaid visit, even with an "unpaid ad" disclaimer, and for a
   creator plugging their own guide, app or channel.

Assumption if unanswered: option 2, with the disclaimer rule either way.

## Scope
- In: the definition in `PostClaimsAgent.getInstructions` (a rule), the schema hint, the references'
  promotional flags redone for posts under the new rule (~199 short Opus calls, about $2), `staleReferences`
  restamped for the rest.
- Out: how reports use the flag.

## Related: a venue's own unlinked account
A post by a venue's account that is not linked to its brand (`ownBrand` unset) often names the venue nowhere
but in the handle. Tried 2026-11-01: a `Posted by: @handle` line in A's input made A list such venues as places
(5 of the 26 new extra places in `mini-v5`), which the references, written without that line, count as wrong.
Deciding whether the handle names the place is part of the same call; if yes, add the line back in
`postClaimsPrompt` and re-reference the posts it touches.

## Acceptance
- [ ] The rule is in A's rules; Opus and gpt-5.4 agree on held-back posts at 90%+ (`reference --model
      openai/gpt-5.4 --as-run`), gpt-mini at 85%+.
