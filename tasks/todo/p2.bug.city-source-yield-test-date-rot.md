# City source yield test rots with the calendar

## Goal
`cities/tests/city.service.pglite.spec.ts` "counts what each source brought in over the last three months" passes on
any day, not only until its seeded posts age out.

## Why
`CityService` counts a source's yield since now minus `YIELD_MONTHS` (3), and the test seeds posts on fixed dates
(`2026-08-10`, `2026-09-10`, …). From 2026-11-10 the August post falls outside the window, so the koelnfood hashtag
reads 1 post and 100 views instead of 2 and 150, and `backend:test` is red for a reason that has nothing to do with the
code. The September posts will follow in December.

## Scope
- In: the test only: seed dates relative to now, or fake `Date` alone (`jest.useFakeTimers({ now, doNotFake: [...] })`
  keeping timers real, since PGlite needs them).
- Out: the window itself (`YIELD_MONTHS`) and the "tags worth scraping" reader, which uses the same window.
