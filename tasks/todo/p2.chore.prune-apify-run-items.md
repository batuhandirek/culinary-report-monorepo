# Prune the stored items of old Apify runs

## Goal
`ApifyRun.items` (jsonb, every actor run's items, stored once since 2026-11-06) is emptied for runs whose callers have long saved what they needed, so the table stays small; the run's row (cost, status, purpose, item count) stays.

## Why
Actor results are large (a review backfill batch is tens of MB) and only the run's caller reads them, once, right after the run. Keeping them forever only costs disk; the reason they are in Postgres at all is to keep them out of DBOS's outputs.

## Scope
- In: a durable schedule (`@DurableSchedule`, daily) that sets `items = null` on finished runs older than N days (assumed 14: a resumed caller still finds them), in batches; its constant; the skill line.
- Out: pruning DBOS's own tables (done: `keepDays` and the `durable-prune` schedule, `durable-workflows` skill; actor runs don't declare it); deleting `apify_runs` rows (the Data page's Apify costs read them).

## Pointers
- `apps/backend/src/modules/apify/entities/apify-run.entity.ts` (`items`)
- `apps/backend/src/modules/apify/services/apify.service.ts` (`finishRun`, `storedItems`, the inline path in `runActor`)
- `apps/backend/src/modules/apify/schedules/apify-credit.schedule.ts` (a schedule in the module, the shape to follow)

## Acceptance
- [ ] Runs finished more than N days ago have `items` null; newer ones keep theirs.
- [ ] A caller resuming within N days still gets its items.

## Open questions
- N: assumed 14 days.
