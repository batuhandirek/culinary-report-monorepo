# Old discovered topics: missing has edges and twins

## Goal
The topics agent D created before the topic graph (`discovered_at` before 2026-11-10) state what their name says they
have ("Vegan döner" has Vegan, "Chicken döner" has Chicken), so every claim naming them counts for that too, and its
twins are one topic each.

## Why
Before the graph, "vegan döner" counted for Vegan through each wording's own `with` link. The conversion made those
wordings exact aliases of the "Vegan döner" topic, which only has `is_a` Döner & kebab, so Vegan lost them: on the
Berlin clone for October 2026 Vegan went 71 → 67 sources, Chicken 562 → 548, Fried chicken 104 → 94, Gemüse kebab
82 → 72. A general rule (a topic takes a `has` most of its old wordings carried) was measured and rejected: it found only
4 cases, all wrong ("Nigerian has Soups"). This is a short admin pass on the topic pages, or a proposal list for the
user to approve.

## Scope
- In: `has` (and missing `is_a`) edges on the old discovered topics, set on `/admin/topics/[key]` ("What it is"), or by
  a reviewed list applied through `TopicService.setEdges`; merging old twins.
- Out: the edges of the topics promoted on 2026-11-10 (they came from their wordings); their near-twins, which no
  spelling rule joins, are in.

## Pointers
- Candidates seen on the clone (each `is_a` only today): Vegan döner (Vegan), Chicken döner (Chicken), Steak döner
  (Beef), Chicken burgers (Chicken), Veggie burgers (Vegetarian), Cheeseburgers (Cheese), Truffle pasta (Truffle),
  Parmesan fries (Parmesan), Garlic sauce (Garlic), Herb sauce (Herbs), Cheese sauce (Cheese), Chocolate sauce
  (Chocolate), Gemüse kebab / Vegetable kebab (Vegetables). With no edge at all: Eggs Benedict, French toast, Waffles,
  Wraps, Granola, Meatballs.
- Twins: Gemüse kebab and Vegetable kebab; Cucumber and Cucumbers; among the promoted, Bao, Bao buns and Baozi (one
  dish under three words).
- `select … from topics where discovered_at < '2026-11-10'` lists them with their edges.

## Acceptance
- [ ] Each candidate has its edges or a note why not; the twins are one topic each.
- [ ] Explore's Vegan count for October 2026 in Berlin is back at 71 or above.
