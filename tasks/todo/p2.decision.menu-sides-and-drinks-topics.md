# Decide whether a menu's sides, desserts and extras get food topics

## Goal
A recorded choice: a menu item that is not a main course (`isMainCourse`: a side, a dessert, a sauce, an extra) keeps giving no food topic, or gives its own name's topics.

## Why
`TopicIndex.resolveMenuItem` gives a non-main course only drink topics, on purpose: "an extra cheese in the pizza section is no pizza", and a menu's topics feed a venue's tags (3+ items and 25 % of the menu) and the topic prices. With menus now read for every report brand, the Menu tab shows "Skinny Fries" and "Kimchi" with no kind of food, though each names one. Taking the name's own topics (never the section's) would link them, at the risk of a dessert-heavy menu tagging a burger place.

## Scope
- In: the choice, and if yes the change in `resolveMenuItem` with its test cases in `topics/tests/topics.spec.ts` rewritten on purpose (they assert the current rule: "Pommes" under "Beilagen" gives nothing).
- Out: main courses (they take the name's topics, and the section's when the name names no dish).

## Pointers
- `apps/backend/src/modules/topics/util/topics.ts` (`resolveMenuItem`), `apps/backend/src/modules/venues/util/delivery.ts` (`isMainCourse`)
- `apps/backend/src/modules/topics/util/venue-topics.ts` (the menu's share in a venue's tags)
