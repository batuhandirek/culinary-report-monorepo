# Decide whether a new branch of a chain counts as a "just opened" post

## Goal
`MentionExtractionAgent`'s `isNewOpening` wording and what mini does agree on new branches, and the openings panel, `newVenueIds` and the lead score treat them the way the user decided.

## Why
The schema says a new branch of an old place is not an opening, but on dev's October posts mini flagged them anyway ("Saravanaa Bhavan … 2nd branch in Berlin is OPEN NOW", "neben Nhas City Chicken … eröffnet noch ein Nhas City Chicken", Sushiyana's larger branch), and re-reading the same post cleared one (Nhas) and kept others. A branch is a venue at its own address, so for the district's openings (Trends panel, the report's neighbourhood line) it is new; for the lead score it isn't (the brand is new only when every branch is, so the bonus stays off either way).

## Scope
- In: the schema description and prompt sentence in `apps/backend/src/modules/social/agents/mention-extraction.agent.ts`; the skill's openings rule; a re-flag of the affected month(s) after a change.
- Out: writing `VenueStatusChange` rows from posts.

## Pointers
- `apps/backend/src/modules/social/agents/mention-extraction.agent.ts` (`isNewOpening`)
- `apps/backend/src/modules/venues/services/venue-status-change.service.ts` (`postOpenings`, `quarter`, `newVenueIds`), `brand-lead-score.service.ts`
- `POST /admin/mentions/reflag-openings` to re-read a month after a wording change

## Acceptance
- [ ] The schema says one thing about new branches, and a re-flag of October shows mini following it on the three posts above.

## Open questions
- Does a new branch of a chain count as an opening? Assumption if unanswered: **yes** (it is a new place at its address; the lead score's brand rule already keeps a chain's bonus off), so the wording drops "or branch" from the exclusions.
