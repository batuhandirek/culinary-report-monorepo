# The `terms` backlog is starved by the reading

## Goal
New food wordings get their topic within a day or two, so what a mention is about (its claims' wordings) and the
topic counts don't go blank for new dishes.

## Why
Agent C (`TermTopicAgent`) is on the free tier only. The nightly drain (22:00 UTC) finds the OpenAI accounts out of
their gpt-5.4-mini allowance (the reading spends it first) and stops with `allowance`: on production every night
from 2026-11-05 to 2026-11-09 did 0 wordings while the queue grew ~150–1,400 a day (5,773 → 7,627 by the backlog's
count; 14,132 undecided on the 2026-11-11 clone). The deploy of the topic graph ships answers for all of those
(`tmp/topic-relinking/`), but the wordings that arrive after it pile up again.

## Scope
- In: how agent C gets served at night.
- Out: the reading's own models.

## Pointers
- Measured 2026-11-11 (`npm run topic-relinking -- sample`, 150 never-decided wordings against Opus): Haiku
  ~$0.00024 a wording, 86 % agreement weighted by uses, but too quick to say "none" (14 of its 36 nones wrong);
  Sonnet ~$0.0038, 7 wrong nones; Opus ~$0.0077.
- `paidFallback` (`BaseMastraAgentConfig`) is how the user lets a named agent move to Claude; `post-gate` on Haiku is
  the precedent.
