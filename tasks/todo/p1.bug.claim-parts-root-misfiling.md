# Agent B files claims at a root when a finer aspect covers them

## Goal
Claims a leaf aspect covers are filed under it, not at its root: "the döner costs 7 euros" under `price_level`,
"it is halal" under `dietary`, "the buffet has fruit" under `menu_variety`.

## Why
- Aspect discovery found ~260 claims of 12,000 sampled loose ones that an existing aspect fits (2026-11-03):
  stated prices → `price_level` ~180, vegan / halal → `dietary` 68, buffet / sauces → `menu_variety` 51,
  "made with love" → `authenticity` 24, bun texture → `cooking` 14. Read fresh (`parts_at` 2026-11-02), with
  `price`'s own hint saying "a price stated for a dish is price level".
- A re-read with the same legend answers the same, so these stay out of the finer counts (reports' themes,
  Explore) until B's instructions change.

## Scope
- In: `ClaimPartsAgent`'s instructions or coaching (a claim goes to the finest aspect that covers it; worked
  examples for stated prices, diets, menu range), then clearing `partsAt` on the root-filed claims so the nightly
  `statement-parts` backlog re-reads them.
- Out: new aspects (`aspect-discovery`); claims about a root as a whole ("the food was good"), filed right.
