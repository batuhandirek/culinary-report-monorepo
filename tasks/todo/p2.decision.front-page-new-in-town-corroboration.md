# Decide whether the front page's New in Town needs the same second signal as the admin's openings

## Goal
The public front page and the admin agree on what "new" means, or differ on purpose, with the reason in the skill.

## Why
Since 2026-10-28 an admin-side opening needs two people's posts or one outside post plus a second signal (`postOpeningsSql`); one post is not enough, since about one flag in five was wrong. The front page's New in Town (`LandingEditionService.openingSigns`) still lists a place from one outside creator's flagged post (own posts are already out). It is the one public page nobody approves before it goes out (`front-page-auto-publish`), so a wrong "new" there is seen by the public. Its wording is safe ("first called new on <day>", never an opening date), and the stricter extraction wording (2026-10-28) already removes the "new spot" promotion, so a single outside post there is a weaker claim than it was.

## Scope
- In: `openingSigns` reading `postOpeningsSql` (with its "first sign ever" rule: the first corroborated date, all time), or keeping it as is and saying why in the skill.
- Out: changing what the admin's openings count.
