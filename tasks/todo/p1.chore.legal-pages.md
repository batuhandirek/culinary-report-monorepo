# Add an Impressum and a privacy notice to the public site

## Goal
`/impressum` and `/datenschutz` (or English equivalents) exist, are linked from the front page's footer and the newsletter form, and say who runs Munchwerk and what is done with the data (the newsletter's addresses and consent, cookies, the creators' posts we count).

## Why
A German site needs an Impressum, and collecting e-mail addresses for a newsletter needs a privacy notice the form can point to. The mockup's footer links both; they were left out because no such pages exist.

## Scope
- In: the two pages (texts from the business, formal, as the legal texts elsewhere come from the backend as they are), the footer links, a line under the newsletter form.
- Out: writing the legal text itself.

## Pointers
- `apps/frontend/src/routes/(landing)/components/LandingFooter.svelte`, `LandingNewsletter.svelte`
- The consent text stored per subscriber: `apps/backend/src/modules/newsletter/constants/newsletter.constant.ts`

## Acceptance
- [ ] Both pages render, are in `sitemap-static.xml`, and are linked from the front page's footer and newsletter form.

## Open questions
- The legal entity, address and contact for the Impressum; the privacy notice's text.
