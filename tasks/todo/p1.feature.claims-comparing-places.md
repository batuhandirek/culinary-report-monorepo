# Claims comparing places

## Goal
"Better than Mustafa's", "the döner here beats the one across the street", "worse than their Kreuzberg branch": the
claim keeps a link to the place it is compared with and which way it goes, so a report can say how guests compare the
owner with competitors (`BrandCompetitor`).

## Why
~125 stored claims compare with another place (2026-11-02, "better than / worse than / compared to"); today the
other place is only words in the text, and it never reaches the competitor section.

## Scope
- In: agent A answers, per claim, the other place it compares with (named as in its text); matching resolves it like a
  mention (`decideMatch`); the claim stores the link and the direction (better / worse / same); the report's
  competitor section; eval cases.
- Out: comparisons with no named place ("better than most kebab places").

## Acceptance
- [ ] A comparing claim links the other place; the eval scores it; the competitor section shows them.
