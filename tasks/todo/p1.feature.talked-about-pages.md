# "Who talked about X"

**Read first**: the brief `statements-and-concepts` (the queries, decision 9, the program's Acceptance), then this.
Needs `statements` and `google-tags-as-statements` done (`reread-sources-with-new-agents` makes the pages worth
looking at). Program order: `topics-tree` → `terms` → `statements` → `reread-sources-with-new-agents` →
`google-tags-as-statements` → **talked-about-pages**.

## Goal
A topic's page (`/admin/trends/[key]`, any kind: Waiting, Price increase, Taste, Pasta, Vegan) answers "who talked
about X" for a city and month: statements whose parts are X or a descendant, across sources (reviews, posts, blog
articles, comments and press when written, Google tags), subjects (brands and branches, the city), sentiments
(praise, complaints, neutral, noticed changes, not judged), with the stated numbers (minutes, euro) and every
sentence newest first; filters by source kind, side, brand, term and food in the URL; "which brands' Google tags
mention X" is the source-kind filter. The theme page (`/admin/trends/themes/[theme]`) and `ThemeInsightService.theme`
fold into it; the Trends "Praise and complaints" tab reads statements. The admin UI for terms (every wording, its
counts, its links to many topics, the agent's suggestions) and for a claim's parts is designed with the user here
(decision 9: "I need to be able to see 'Waiting Time', 'Price Increase', 'Taste'"). The program's brief closes with
this task: its Analysis is folded into the skill.

## Why
This is the query the whole program exists for (the brief's "Why", in the user's words). Until it exists the
statements are only a better store.

## Scope
- In: the topic page's new sections and filters, the service behind them (`StatementInsightService`, generalising
  `ThemeInsightService.theme` + `TopicInsightService.detail`), the themes tab over statements, the Terms tab (design
  first), a claim's parts on a post's and a review's page with correction if the user wants it (an admin's word
  final per claim, like a mention's), the `feedback-theme-discovery` task reshaped (clusters over statements with no
  aspect or filed at a root; "Add as topic" creates an aspect row, no commit needed), tests, the skill, closing the
  brief.
- Out: public pages, the portal (a later task if the owner should see "what guests say about X"), new sources.

## Order of work
1. Design with the user, before code: what the topic page shows for an aspect vs a food (sketch both with dev data),
   the Terms tab's columns and actions, whether a claim's parts are editable; record the answers in this task.
2. `StatementInsightService.topic(key, { cityId, month, side?, kind?, brandId?, term?, food?, offset, limit })`
   over `statement_topics` with descendants, a source once per topic and side; the figures (shares of written
   reviews and of posts by others over six months, brands, terms, foods, stated amounts, Google-tag brands) and the
   sentences; pure SQL + a pure `statement-detail.ts`; `GET /admin/topic-insights/:key` extended, the theme routes
   removed.
3. The page: the topic page gains "Who talked about it" (figures, filters, sentences) under its existing panels; the
   theme page route redirects or goes; `themeHref` → the topic page; the Trends themes tab over
   `StatementInsightService.city` (was `ThemeInsightService.city`).
4. The Terms tab as designed; a claim's parts as designed.
5. `feedback-theme-discovery` rewritten to statements (its design is otherwise sound).
6. Tests: `statement-insight.pglite.spec.ts` (descendants, every source kind, subjects incl. the city, sides incl.
   neutral and not judged, amounts, filters narrowing the sentences only, paging, a Google-tag brand list), the
   routes; `svelte-check`.
7. Close the program: the brief's Analysis → the skill (entities, invariants, the one entry points, the agents,
   the backlogs, the pages); the brief and this task to `done/`, then deleted once folded.
