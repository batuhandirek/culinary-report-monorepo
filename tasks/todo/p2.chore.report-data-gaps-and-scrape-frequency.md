# Close the data gaps the report analysis runs into by raising scrape frequencies

## Goal
Each gap below is either closed by a more frequent (or wider) scrape, or accepted with its cost written down. Afterwards the analysis's playbooks answer their questions at week resolution for the metrics an owner asks "when?" about.

## Why
The report analysis (`p1.feature.report-analysis-hypotheses`) explains a move by looking at the days or weeks around it. Where a figure is sampled once a month, a within-month move is invisible: the playbook answers "unknown" and the admin trace says why, but nothing in the owner's report does (the user's call: gaps are a task, not UI). The pipeline's recurring crons already run weekly or daily for posts; the place and account figures lag behind.
