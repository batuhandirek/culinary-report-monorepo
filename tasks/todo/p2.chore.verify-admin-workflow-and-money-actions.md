# Check the action buttons on the Workflows and Money pages

## Goal
Every action on `/admin/workflows` and `/admin/money` does what its button says, on the dev stack.

## Why
The pages shipped with their listings checked against the dev database; Cancel and Fork were clicked once, the rest not.

## Scope
- In: Resume on a cancelled or failed run; Fork from a later step (the steps before it copied); Trigger, Pause, Resume and the undo toast on the Schedules tab (needs written schedules: `INGESTION_IS_DISABLED=false` for the session, or a schedule applied by hand; watch what the dev backend then starts, it may spend); the Credits tab's manual snapshot form (stale when the cycle end is past) and Apify's Refresh.
- Out: new actions.

## Pointers
- `apps/frontend/src/routes/(admin)/admin/workflows/[id]/+page.svelte`, `…/workflows/components/SchedulesTab.svelte`, `…/money/components/CreditsTab.svelte`, `SnapshotForm.svelte`
- A free real run: enqueue `ApifyCreditSchedule.snapshot` on `munchwerk-development.apify` with a `DBOSClient` (`appVersion: 'munchwerk-development-v1'`; add `delaySeconds` for a run that waits, to cancel); it reads the Apify account only.

## Acceptance
- [ ] Resume runs a cancelled run on; Fork from step N copies the steps before N.
- [ ] Trigger starts a run and links to it; Pause and Resume flip the badge; the toast's Undo reverses.
- [ ] A manual snapshot shows on its provider's card; Apify's Refresh writes a new one.
