# Read deals and promotions as claims

## Goal
A deal a place announces or a creator passes on ("Baklava for only 20 € a kilo, 15–25 March", "all wraps 7 € in April
with free ayran", happy hour, 2-for-1, a free drink with a student card, an opening discount) is a claim about that
place under one aspect for deals, with the item as a food wording, the price as its amount, and the period it runs,
so we can list per city and month who runs which deal, for what, and when.

## Why
Deals are a food-culture signal (what is pushed, at what price, in which season) and a sales hook (a place running
deals is spending on promotion). Today they are claims under `price_level` at best, with no period: "Vom 15.03 bis zum
25.03 Baklava für nur 20 € das Kilo" by Köşgeroğlu's own account (dev Berlin) reads as a price, not as a deal.
Not to confuse with `SocialPost.isPromotional`, which is a paid partnership or gifted meal (the creator promoted),
not a deal the place offers.

## Scope
- In: agent A (`PostClaimsAgent`) keeping a deal as one claim with its period in the text ("baklava 20 € a kilo from
  15 to 25 March"); an aspect for it (assumption: a new leaf under `price`, `deal`: "a discount, happy hour, 2-for-1, a
  free item, a deal with dates"; aspects are rows, added by the admin topic dialog or a seed migration); agent B filing
  it there with the price as `amount`; the period (open question below); a list "deals" (city × month: place, item,
  price, period, own post or not, source) as a Claims table filter or a Trends view; tests.
- Out: delivery platforms' discounts (their menus, a separate source), reviews mentioning a past deal (they are claims
  already, they just get the aspect), reports and the front page using deals (later).

## Open questions
- The period: kept in the claim's text only (an atom, readable, not queryable), or two columns on `Statement`
  (`validFrom`, `validTo`, read by agent B). Assumption: text only first; columns once a list needs "deals running this
  week".
- Whether a permanent "lunch menu 9.90 €" is a deal or a price. Assumption: a price (`price_level`); a deal has a
  limit (dates, hours, a condition).
