# On the house: who gave what

## Goal
A city's public month page lists the places that gave guests something on the house ("they brought us çay and
baklava", "the owner gave us a shot after dinner"), what they gave and how many guests said so. It reads the claims
filed under the `on_the_house` aspect and their food parts.

## Why
It's a warm, positive public story (hospitality, Turkish çay ikramı, the dessert after the bill) that names venues
only for something good, so it fits the brand's "never a negative list" rule. It also tells a venue something its
guests notice.

## Scope
- In:
  - Check the aspect's hint against `KIND_RULES` (`deal` says "a free item"). "Free ayran with every wrap" is a
    condition the place advertises: `kind = deal`, and the list leaves it out even if it is filed under
    `on_the_house`.
  - The list's rules:
    - The aspect is `on_the_house` and the kind is not `deal`, `question` or `event`.
    - Leave out promotional posts and ads (`isPromotional`, `isAd`): a gifted meal for a creator is promotion.
    - Leave out own posts (`ownPostSql`): a place saying "free tea for everyone" is marketing.
    - The "what" is the claim's food parts (term → topic label). A claim with no food part counts for the place
      without an item.
  - The admin view: the Claims tables already filter by aspect, so check that it works for `on_the_house`.
  - The public view: a section on the city's month page (`CityHighlightData`, which an admin builds and publishes).
    Per place it shows the place, the items in our own words (the topic label, or the term) and the number of
    different reviews or posts. Leave out places and creators on the do-not-list (`isDeniedVenue`, `isDeniedCreator`),
    and keep a minimum count so one wrong claim never makes a public line.
  - Tests.
- Out:
  - Review text or quotes in public (brand guardrail: never republish review texts or ratings).
  - The front page's live edition (it publishes without approval; add the list later if wanted).
  - The quarterly trends page.
  - The venue's private report (a later line in its "worth your attention" cards).
