# Give every account stored before user ids its id, by reading one of its posts again

## Goal
Every Instagram and TikTok account that posted has its platform user id, and every one renamed since we first saw it is one account with the rename kept, so a creator's page says "was @old" and no person is split in two.

## Why
Ids are stored since 2026-11-04 (`SocialAccount.platformUserId`); an account last seen posting before then has none, and a rename of it reads as a new account and a new creator. On the dev copy (2026-11-14): 361 Instagram and 1,988 TikTok accounts with posts have no id; `happeninginberlin` (creator `0825a22a-af93-4b3c-9a82-94ecf4e85855`, posts until 2026-11-03) is `berlinbitte` (id `46232504208`, its own creator since 2026-11-05), the same numbered "N Berlin spots" captions, and our profile reads of `happeninginberlin` on 10–11 November came back empty. Stored Apify items can't fill them: runs before ~2026-11-10 kept no items. Wait for the Apify cycle to reset on 18 November (`restore-step-budgets-after-apify-reset` is the same day; the credit is kept for the post streams until then).

## Scope
- In: a one-off durable run (started by hand, never a cron) that takes, per city, every TikTok and Instagram account with a post and no `platformUserId` (not lost, `handleLostAt` null), picks its newest stored post, reads those posts by URL with the counts refresh's actors (`apify/instagram-scraper` `directUrls`, `resultsType: 'posts'`, `resultsLimit: 1`, ~$0.0027 a post; `clockworks/tiktok-scraper` `postURLs`, ~$0.0039 a post, cap ≥ $0.50), each run a child on the `apify` queue, and hands each item's author to `SocialAccountService.authorOfStoredPost` (already built: an id-less account whose post comes back under another handle is merged into the id's account with the rename kept; under the same handle it takes the id). Don't touch the post's counts (`readCounts` would move a final post's counts; only the author is wanted). A post that comes back deleted or private proves nothing: try the account's next newest post once, else leave it.
- Out: blogs (no id); accounts with no post (captions, sites, outreach: they get an id when first seen posting); the creator merge of two creators an admin already split (`absorb` follows the accounts; an admin's split creator is left as it is).

## Pointers

- `apps/backend/src/modules/social/services/post-counts.service.ts` (the same two actor calls and the item → post mapping by id and URL; `save` already passes the author)
- `apps/backend/src/modules/pipeline/post-counts/` (the operation's workflow shape to copy: plan step, actor children, save step)

- Cost on the dev copy, 2026-11-14: 361 × $0.0027 ≈ $1 Instagram, 1,988 × $0.0039 ≈ $7.75 TikTok, ~$9; recount at the start, production may differ.
