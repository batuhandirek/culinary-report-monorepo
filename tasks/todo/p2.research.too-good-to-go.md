# Research: Too Good To Go listings

**Priority: the lowest. Pick up only when nothing else is queued.**

## Goal
Know whether, and how cheaply, we can read which of the city's venues sell Too Good To Go surprise bags, at what
price and pickup times, with what rating, and whether that is worth anything in a report or on a city page.

## Questions
1. Where the data comes from: an Apify actor (cost on the Free plan, `apify-free-plan-only`), a public endpoint, or
   nothing usable (terms of use).
2. What a listing gives: store name and address (to match to our venues by name and place, `decideMatch`-style),
   bag kind, price and original value, pickup window, rating, how often bags are offered or sold out.
3. Where it would land, consolidating first: venue details (`Venue.details`) or a dated snapshot, not a new entity
   unless nothing fits.
4. What it is for: a signal in the venue's report (surplus, pricing), a city-page note, the lead score.
