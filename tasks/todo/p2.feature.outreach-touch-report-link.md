# Outreach touch → report link

## Goal
A touch that sent a report (the report e-mail, or a pitch with a report link) points at that `Report` and the `ReportShare` used. A brand's Sales tab shows next to the touch whether the link was opened, how often, and when last.

## Why
- `ReportDeliveryService.send` (`outreach/services/report-delivery.service.ts`, ~line 90) logs the touch with `body: props.shareUrl`: the link is only text in the body. The report is reachable only by parsing that URL.
- `ReportShare` already counts opens (`openCount`, `lastOpenedAt`), but nothing connects them to the prospect's outreach log.
- `ensureShare` reuses one live link per report, so the admin's copied link and the e-mailed one share a counter. Opens can't be told apart per send.

## Design
- `OutreachTouch.report` → `Report` (nullable, `set null`) and `OutreachTouch.share` → `ReportShare` (nullable, `set null`).
- `ReportDeliveryService.send` creates a share of its own for each send (`reportShareService.create(report.id)`, `createdBy` null), not `ensureShare`, and passes `report` and `share` to `OutreachService.log` (input gains optional `reportId` / `shareId`, validated to belong to the prospect's brand). The body keeps the URL for reading.
- The composer on the Sales tab, when an admin logs a touch that includes a report link, can attach the report. A picker of the brand's reports makes a fresh share, as above. Check the `domain-outreach` skill for the composer's flow.
- Output: the touch DTO carries `report { id, month }` and `share { openCount, lastOpenedAt, revokedAt }`. The Sales tab shows "opened 3×, last 2 days ago" or "not opened".

## Scope
- In: the two relations, the delivery path, the composer's attach option, the Sales tab line, tests, both skills.
- Out: open tracking for e-mails themselves (pixels), and prospect stage changes driven by opens.
