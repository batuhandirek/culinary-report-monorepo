# Show a claim's menu item where the claim is shown, and the menu in the report

## Goal
A claim whose food wording means one of the brand's menu items says so wherever it is drawn (the review page, the claims tables, the post page: the item's name and price beside the wording), and a report can show the brand's most named menu items with what was said about each and what changed on the menu in the month.

## Why
The link exists (`MenuItemTerm`, read by the brand's Menu tab only). A review reading "the banging bangkok burger was dry" shows a plain wording today; the owner's report says nothing about which menu items were praised, added or removed.

## Scope
- In: the menu item on `StatementOutputDto`'s food parts (joined at read time by the claim's brand, never stored on the claim), drawn by the shared claim components.
- In: a report section kind for the menu: the most named items with praise and complaints, items added and removed in the month (`firstSeenAt`, `removedAt`), price moves (`MenuItemSnapshot`, a row a month).
- Out: the close calls the code can't link (task `menu-item-wordings-close-calls`).

## Pointers
- `apps/backend/src/modules/venues/services/brand-menu.service.ts` (`namings`: the join to copy)
- `apps/backend/src/modules/statements/util/statement-populate.ts`, `apps/frontend/src/lib/statements.ts`
- `apps/backend/src/modules/reports/util/report-sections.ts`

## Acceptance
- [ ] A review's page shows the menu item beside a linked wording.
- [ ] A built report with the section lists the month's most named items and the menu's changes.
