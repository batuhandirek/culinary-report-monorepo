# Consolidate the topic vocabularies

## Goal
One vocabulary (the `Topic` tree) and one wording layer (`Term`) everywhere: nothing keeps its own list of what things
can be about, and every wording is decided once.

## Why
The model is mostly there (one `Topic` tree, `Term` + `TermTopic`, link tables per kind of thing), but two spots still
run on their own: comment questions have an enum of their own, and mention cuisines and menu item names are matched
to topics by a separate resolver into their own link tables, outside the term decisions.

## Scope
- In:
  1. `PostCommentQuestionTopicEnum` (location, price, hours, menu, dietary, payment, reservation, other) → the aspect a
     question is about (a `Topic` of kind aspect; `CommentIntentAgent` answers a key checked against the index, like
     agent B), its readers (`ReportData.comments`, the report's question topics) moved; or comment questions become
     statements of source kind `comment` (the brief planned the column) with B filing them. Decide which first.
  2. Evaluate, then do if it holds: a mention's cuisine text and a menu item's name as `Term`s with their `TermTopic`
     links read at query time (as claims' food parts are), replacing `MentionTopic` / `MenuItemTopic` and their re-link
     step. Watch the volume (menu items: thousands of names for agent C), the `source` cuisine | category distinction and
     the cuisine's order (rank) that `MentionTopic` keeps, and `VenueTopic`'s inputs.
  3. Rename `Mention.cuisine` (and the agent's field, DTOs, readers, the admin labels): it holds any food words the post
     gives for the place ("Pho, Vietnamese", "Vegan, Döner & kebab"), not cuisines; e.g. `foodWords`. With (2) it is the
     source of the mention's terms. The column rename is a hand-written migration (`rename column`, see `backend-entity`).
- Out: `VenueTopic` (computed tags plus admin decisions: its own data), the "theme" → "aspect" rename (done with
  `talked-about-pages`).
