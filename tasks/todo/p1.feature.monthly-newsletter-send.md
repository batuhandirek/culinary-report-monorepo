# Send the monthly newsletter to the front page's confirmed addresses

## Goal
On the 1st of every month each confirmed, not unsubscribed address of a city gets one e-mail: the month's Heat Check top 10, the dishes, what opened, with a one-tap unsubscribe link (`/newsletter/unsubscribe?token=`).

## Why
The front page promises "In your inbox on the 1st" and collects addresses with double opt-in (`NewsletterSubscriber`), but nothing sends to the list yet.

## Scope
- In: a `NewsletterEmail` template (en), a monthly cron and job per city that sends to confirmed addresses only, in batches, recording what went to whom (a sent row per subscriber and month, never a column on the subscriber), the unsubscribe link and a `List-Unsubscribe` header, the content from the newest published monthly highlight or the front page's edition.
- Out: other cities' editions beyond what the city rows give, a marketing tool or tracking pixels.

## Pointers
- `apps/backend/src/modules/newsletter/` (`NewsletterService`, `NewsletterSubscriber`)
- `packages/emails/src/emails/NewsletterConfirmEmail.tsx` (the style), `modules/email/services/email.service.ts`
- `modules/highlights` (`CityHighlightService.published`), `modules/landing` (`LandingEditionService.published`)

## Acceptance
- [ ] A PGlite test: only confirmed, not unsubscribed addresses get the month's mail, once; the mail carries the unsubscribe link.
- [ ] The Front page tab shows the last send.

## Open questions
- Which content leads: the published monthly Heat Check (approved by an admin) or the live edition? Assumed: the monthly page, since it is approved.
- Brevo: a marketing sender and list setup is needed before the first send.
