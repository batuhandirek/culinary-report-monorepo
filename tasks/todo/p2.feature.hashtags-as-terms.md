# Link hashtags to topics through terms, so agent C decides what a tag names

## Goal
Every hashtag the city's posts carry is a wording that can point at topics the way a claim's food
wording does: `#smashburger`, `#berlincafe`, `#veganberlin` and `#pidelove` say what a post is
about even when its claims don't. A tag's topics come from a term decided once (a name match, agent
C, or an admin), not only from a whole-name match against the topics.

## Why
Today `HashtagService.relink` links a tag to a topic only when the tag *is* a topic's name or an
exact term (`TopicIndex.exact`). On dev Berlin (2026-11-11) 454 of 15,470 tags had a topic and 893 a
brand. Compounds with the city or a word ("berlincafe", "veganberlin", "ramenlover") link nothing.
Since a mention's kinds of food now come only from its claims ("What a mention is about is what its
claims name" in the skill), a tag is the other signal a post gives about food that no claim says.

## Scope
- In: hashtags stored as terms (or pointing at one) so the `terms` backlog decides them; agent C
  told it is reading a hashtag (city names and filler like "fyp", "foodtok" are none); the city's
  own name and aliases stripped before the decision ("berlincafe" → café); `Hashtag.topic` (or its
  replacement) read from the decision; the hashtag rows on a post's page showing it.
- Out: counting posts by their tags in the trends (a separate decision: tags are noisier than
  claims); brand links (`Hashtag.brand` stays as it is).

## Pointers
- `apps/backend/src/modules/social/services/hashtag.service.ts` (`relink`, the one writer)
- `apps/backend/src/modules/social/util/hashtags.ts` (`hashtagLinks`, `hashtagKey`)
- `apps/backend/src/modules/topics/services/term.service.ts` (`sync` stores Google tags and categories as terms the same way)
- `apps/backend/src/modules/topics/agents/term-topic.agent.ts` (agent C)

## Acceptance
- [ ] A tag like `#berlincafe` ends up linked to Café after the `terms` backlog runs; `#fyp` and `#berlin` link nothing.
- [ ] An admin's decision on a tag's wording moves the tag's link at the next re-link.
- [ ] A PGlite test covers both.

## Open questions
- Should a tag's topics also count what a mention is about when its claims name no food? Assumption: no, claims only, until the user decides after seeing the links.
