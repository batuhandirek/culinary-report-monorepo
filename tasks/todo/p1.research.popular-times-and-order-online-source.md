# Find a source for Google popular times and "Order online" links; ours return none

## Goal
Know whether busyness (`SAMPLE_BUSYNESS`, the report's "Did the buzz fill the place?") and Wolt menus (`SCRAPE_DELIVERY`, prices) can get data at all, and from which actor or source, or drop them from the monthly run and the report until they can.

## Why
No venue in the dev database has ever had `popularTimesHistogram`, `orderBy` or `peopleAlsoSearch` in `Venue.details` (4,780 venues with details; checked 2026-10-19). `compass/crawler-google-places` with `scrapePlaceDetailPage: true` returned `popularTimesHistogram: {}`, `peopleAlsoSearch: []` and no `orderBy` for all 127 report venues and competitors of September, and again for Döner Pro and Choc-Spot with `scrapeOrderOnline: true` (a test run, $0.01). So `SAMPLE_BUSYNESS` pays for samples with `histogram: null`, the busyness section and the Wolt menus never appear, competitor suggestions never get the "people also search" bonus, and `SCRAPE_DELIVERY` falls back to Lieferando's city listing for every venue. The same detail pages do return `reviewsTags` (Google's "people often mention"), now stored.

## Scope
- In: one small paid test of each candidate (another Google Maps actor that returns popular times, the same actor with a residential proxy or `language: 'de'`, Google's own "Popular times" through the plain-fetch route `GoogleReserveAvailabilityService` uses); the cost per venue; a recommendation.
- Out: building the replacement (its own task once a source works).

## Pointers
- `venues/services/venue-scrape.service.ts` (`refreshDetails`, `scrapeDetailPages`), `venues/util/venue-details.ts`
- `venues/services/venue-busyness.service.ts`, `venues/services/venue-delivery.service.ts`

## Acceptance
- [ ] A source that returns popular times for 5 of 10 busy Berlin venues, or a recorded "none works", with costs.
- [ ] The same for delivery links.
- [ ] If none: a follow-up task to take busyness out of the monthly cron and the report.

## Open questions
- Is busyness worth a paid actor at all? Assumption: only if under ~$0.01 a venue-sample.
