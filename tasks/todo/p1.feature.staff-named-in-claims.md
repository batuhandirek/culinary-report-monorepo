# Staff named in claims

## Goal
A claim that names a staff member ("our waitress Alena looked after us", "thanks to Ella and the team", "Tolga served
us") keeps that name as a part of its own, beside its aspect (friendliness, attentiveness …), so a venue's report can
say who guests name and how: "Alena: 6 mentions, all positive".

## Why
One aspect a claim: "Alena was very friendly" is friendliness, so the name is lost to every count. A strict search of
the stored claims found 50+ that name staff (2026-11-02), more once claims are re-read; owners use it to thank and coach staff.

## Scope
- In: agent B (or a specialist, one claim a call) answers the people a claim names and their role (waiter, chef,
  owner, staff); a `StatementPart` role for a person (consolidate with the food part's shape: a wording kept as said,
  linked later), never a new entity unless nothing fits; the venue report's line; eval cases.
- Out: identifying the same person across venues; anything public (names stay in the owner's report).

## Open questions
- Does a creator's name (the TikTok author) count? Assumption: no, only the venue's people.

## Acceptance
- [ ] A claim naming a staff member stores the name and role; the eval scores it.
- [ ] The venue's report lists the names with counts and sentiment.
