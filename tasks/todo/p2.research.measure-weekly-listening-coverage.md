# Measure whether weekly listening covers a month's TikTok posts

## Goal
Know whether November 2026 (the first month the Sunday `SCRAPE_TIKTOK_RECENT` runs covered in full) has its TikTok posts by the 2nd of December, or whether the weekly budgets or page sizes need changing.

## Why
`continuous-listening` replaced the single $15 month-end TikTok scrape with weekly runs ($6 cap, ~$2.30 spent) plus a $7 month-end pass, on the assumption that a weekly short page per hashtag and a `WEEK` search find most of a month. That has only been checked by tests, not on real data.

## Scope
- In: after December's monthly run, compare the Data page's November TikTok count before the month-end `SCRAPE_TIKTOK` with what it added (the step result's `saved`), and the Apify spend of November's weekly runs. Acceptance from the original task: the weekly runs' count is within 20 % of what the month-end search adds on top. Adjust `perSource` / budgets in `pipeline.service.ts` and `pipeline.constant.ts` if not.
- Out: changing actors.

## Pointers
- `/admin/data` (Months tab: November's TikTok posts; Runs tab: the `SCRAPE_TIKTOK_RECENT` runs and their Apify costs)
- `apps/backend/src/modules/pipeline/crons/weekly-listening.cron.ts`, `pipeline/constants/pipeline.constant.ts` (`SCRAPE_TIKTOK_RECENT` $6, `SCRAPE_TIKTOK` $7)
- `apify_runs` rows with purpose `tiktok hashtags …` / `tiktok search …` for November

## Acceptance
- [ ] November's TikTok count from the weekly runs and the month-end pass's `saved` recorded here, with the month's Apify spend on TikTok.
- [ ] Budgets left as they are or changed, with the reason in the log and the skill's weekly-listening rule updated.

## Open questions
None (measurement only; a budget change above ~$15 a month for TikTok goes to the user).
