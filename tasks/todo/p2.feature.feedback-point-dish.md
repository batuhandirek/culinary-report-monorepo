# Dish on feedback points

## Goal
A point about one dish carries it: "the kebab was cold" is `{ theme: 'temperature', dish: 'kebab', detail }`. The city's Praise and complaints tab breaks a theme down by dish, grouped like the city's dish table, so "Döner & kebab: cold 14, small portions 9" holds across spellings and venues. A point not about one dish has none.

## Why
Today a point stores topic, theme and phrase. Dishes are a separate list per review or post, with no link to the point. Of 9,192 food points in reviews on dev, 4,084 (44 %) name a dish of the same review in their phrase. Matching by phrase covers less than half and is thin per pair: the biggest complaint pair over every month is "meat" under Cooking, 16 points at 11 venues. The kind-of-food breakdown (`brandFoods`) answers "what do döner places get criticised for", not "which dish": a burger place's cold fries count under burgers.

## Design
- `feedbackPointSchema` (`venues/agents/review-analysis.agent.ts`) gets an optional `dish`: the dish or drink the point is about, as written in the review's `dishes` list, absent when the point is about the place or the food as a whole. `FEEDBACK_THEMES_INSTRUCTION` says so in one sentence. Both agents answer it: `ReviewAnalysisAgent` and `MentionExtractionAgent`. `FeedbackPoint` gets `dish?: string`.
- Backfill for stored points: `FeedbackThemeAgent` sees only the phrase, so a second pass `FeedbackDishAgent` (mini) gets the phrase plus the source's `dishes` names and picks one or none. Points only, never extracting a review or post again. It is like `FeedbackThemeService.backfillReviews` but needs a marker for "looked at, no dish" (`dish: null` stored), so it doesn't repeat. Cost on dev: ~27k points in ~230 calls, the size of the theme backfill (6 minutes).
- Grouping: the dish name goes through the same rule as the city's dish table (`dishRow` in `trends/util/compute-trends.ts`: a food-topic `dish`/`drink` alias first, else `dishKey`), so "döner", "doner kebab" and "kebabs" are one row.
- City counts: `countThemes` gets a per-theme `dishes` (top five, sources and share of that dish's sources with a point), in the opened row next to brands and kinds of food. Counted once per source, like everything else.
- Report: out of scope here (see Scope).

## Scope
- In: the schema field on both agents, the backfill, the grouping, the city tab breakdown.
- Out: the venue report (its dish list and theme rows wait for the per-branch report work); a "dish × theme" page of its own; comparing a dish across months.
