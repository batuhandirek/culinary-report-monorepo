# Restore Berlin's paused step budgets after the Apify cycle resets, and fix the city page's budget list

## Goal
From 18 November Berlin's crons run on their default budgets again and the paused one-offs are redone, before the monthly run of 2 December; and the city page lists every paid step with its real default, so budgets can be set there instead of through the API.

## Why
On 2026-11-05 (production, `beta.munchwerk.com`) Berlin's `stepBudgetsUsd` was set through `PATCH /admin/cities/:id` to keep the $38 left for the post streams (Instagram hashtags, watched creators, the Sunday TikTok search and reels, the brand-name TikTok search):
`REFRESH_POST_COUNTS`, `SNAPSHOT_RATINGS`, `TRACK_PROFILES`, `FILL_ACCOUNT_FOLLOWERS`, `SCRAPE_ACCOUNTS_RECENT`, `LOOKUP_VENUES`, `FIND_OWN_ACCOUNTS`, `SAMPLE_BUSYNESS`, `TRACK_SEARCH_RANKS` all `0`, and `dishSearchEveryDays` `0` (was 30; the map was empty before, every step on its default). Left at 0, the monthly run of 2 December would skip its search ranks and the weekly crons stay off. The city page could not do it: `CityFormDialog.svelte` lists a stale set of steps with old fallbacks (no `REFRESH_POST_COUNTS`, `FILL_ACCOUNT_FOLLOWERS`, `TRACK_PROFILES`, `SCRAPE_CREATORS`, `SCRAPE_VENUE_SOCIAL_RECENT`; "Snapshot ratings, twice a week $3", "Track search ranks $3", "Watched accounts, weekly $2" against today's 15, 10, 16).

## Scope
- In: on or after 18 November, clear the paused keys (`stepBudgetsUsd: {}` restores every default) and set `dishSearchEveryDays` back to 30; check the Apify cycle's credit first (`GET /v2/users/me/limits`).
- In: redo what was paused: `TRACK_SEARCH_RANKS` for 2026-10 from the Data page (the one-off `search-ranks-one-run-each` ran at $0); the dish snapshot follows by itself once the interval is back; `BACKFILL_REVIEWS` (since 2026-05-01, talked days 90; paused at ~$57, ~600 venues) when the user says yes to its spend (~$80+ left at the measured $3–17 a batch of 100).
- In: the city form's budget list from one source with the backend's `DEFAULT_STEP_BUDGET_USD` (every paid step, labels from `lib/pipeline-steps.ts`), not a hand-kept copy.
- Out: changing default budgets.
