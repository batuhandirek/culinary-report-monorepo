# Re-file a group's claims into the aspect created from it

## Goal
When an admin creates an aspect from a "Missing aspects" group (Trends tab), the claims of that group that are
filed at a root or under no aspect are read again by agent B, so they move into the new aspect without a re-read
of their sources.

## Why
- Agent B reads only claims with `partsAt` null. A new aspect is offered to B from the next read on, but the
  claims that showed the gap keep their root (or no aspect) until something clears their `partsAt`. So the
  group that justified the aspect never counts under it, and the tab keeps showing it.
- Clearing `partsAt` is cheap (mini, the nightly `statement-parts` backlog) and loses nothing (skill: "Clearing
  `partsAt` re-reads a claim without touching its source").

## Scope
- In: an admin action ("Re-read these claims", or done with "Create aspect") that clears `partsAt` on the claims
  of the scope closest to the group (the group's ids as worked out, or a `like` search above a cosine floor),
  only those with no aspect or a root; the statement-parts backlog does the rest. The clusters' memory for the
  scope is dropped.
- Out: filing by vector (agent B decides); re-reading claims under finer aspects.

## Pointers
- `apps/backend/src/modules/statements/services/statement-cluster.service.ts` (groups are not stored: the ids
  would have to come back with the answer, or be found again)
- `apps/backend/src/modules/statements/services/statement-parts.service.ts` (`PARTS_PENDING_SQL`)
- `apps/frontend/src/routes/(admin)/admin/trends/components/TrendsMissingAspectsTab.svelte`, `+page.svelte`
  (`TopicFormDialog` `onSaved`)
