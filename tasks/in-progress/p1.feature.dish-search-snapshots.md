# Dish searches as dated snapshots, on a per-city interval

## Goal
`TRACK_DISH_SEARCHES` (every dish topic × every district, the Google Maps top ten) is a dated snapshot of the day it ran, taken every `City.dishSearchEveryDays`, with the day split out for SQL.

## Done (2026-10-25)
- `DishSearchRank.searchedOn` (date, the city's local day) plus `year`, `month`, `monthOfYear`, `dayOfMonth`, `dayOfYear`, `isoYear`, `isoWeek`, `weekday` (`calendarDay` in `venues/util/week.ts`); hand-written `Migration20261025121206_dish_search_snapshots`, applied on dev.
- `City.dishSearchEveryDays` (default 30, 0 off) in the entity, DTOs and the city form, with the cost per cadence in its hint.
- `DishSearchCron` (daily 05:15 UTC) queues a run when a city's newest snapshot is that old; dish searches left the monthly cron.
- `GET /admin/dish-searches` takes `searchedOn` (newest by default) and returns `snapshots` and `previousOn`; the topic panel has a snapshot picker; the branch panel's columns are snapshot dates.
- Tests: `dish-searches.spec.ts`, `dish-search.pglite.spec.ts`, `dish-search-cron.pglite.spec.ts`; skill and `human-docs` 02 / 03 / 08 / 10 updated.
- Live on dev (restarted 15:09): `GET /admin/dish-searches` returns the 25 Oct snapshot, a branch's searches come back by date, `cron.dish-searches` is scheduled at 05:15 UTC.
- The first full snapshot, Berlin 2026-10-25: 47 dishes, 528 of 564 searches came back (the rest found nothing, most of them hot pot (found in 2 districts), malatang (4) and poke (7)), 3,708 places, 3,572 of them our venues; $14.83.
