# Rebuild the admin around brands, with a Today page and a data coverage view

## Goal
The admin reads like the product: a **brand** is the unit everything hangs off (its branches, posts, reviews, competitors, reports, signals and the sales conversation), the home page says **what to do today**, and one page shows **which months are complete** and lets an admin finish the gaps.

## Why
The data model already made the brand the unit (`brand-as-unit`), but the admin still grew one page per backend module: Venues (4,555 rows, sorted by all-time mentions dominated by an old March scrape), Brands (only the 4 chains), Outreach (its own list of the same brands), Topics and Trends (two views of the same cuisine shares), Pipeline (a log of runs, no notion of a month being done). Answering "how is Döner Pro doing, and what do I do about it?" meant five pages.

## The pillars and where each lives

| Pillar | Page | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Cities and districts | **Cities** | sources, districts, calendar (as today) |
| Brands and venues | **Brands** (list of *every* brand) → brand page (the hub) → branch page | a venue is a branch of a brand; never a top-level list |
| Food topics | **Trends** (topics, dishes, districts, creators, rising, openings, unrecognised words) | Topics and Trends merged |
| Social posts | **Posts** | the listening feed; each post links to the brands it names |
| Data points (reservations, busyness, delivery, ranks, press, accounts) | the brand's **Signals** tab; coverage on **Data** | |
| Reports | **Reports** + the brand's Reports tab | |
| Selling | the brand's **Sales** tab; the Brands list's Prospects / Customers segments; follow-ups on **Today** | no Outreach menu entry |
| Running the pipeline | **Data**: month × step coverage, runs, Apify spend | was Pipeline |

Sidebar: Today · *Market*: Brands, Posts, Trends, Cities · *Output*: Reports · *Operations*: Data · *Content*: Blog · *People*: Users, Sessions · *System*: UI kit.

## The brand list (`/admin/brands`)
Every brand of a city for a month (default: the report month, `lastMonth()`), one row each: name with its kind of food and district (or "N branches"), posts by others and change on the month before, views, rating, lead score, sales stage, the month's report. Segments: Talked about (default: posts this month), All, Prospects, Customers (won), Chains, Merge suggestions (the old Suggested tab). Search, district and food filters, sortable.

## The brand page (`/admin/brands/[id]`)
Header: name, food, branches, cities, stage, lead score; month picker; Build report; Add to sales / stage.
Tabs: **Overview** (the month in numbers with changes, what needs doing, the latest report's headline, lead score, branches at a glance) · **Posts** (every branch's mentions and the brand-only ones) · **Reviews** (every branch) · **Branches** (each branch's figures; branch tools: find, possible branches, split) · **Competitors** · **Signals** (open tables, busyness, delivery, search ranks, press, own accounts) · **Reports** · **Sales** (the prospect workspace) · **Settings** (report scope, own accounts).

The **branch page** (`/admin/venues/[id]`) keeps only what belongs to the place: Google facts and details, food topics, demand, search ranks, rating over time, the branch's posts and reviews. Its header names the brand and links back.

## Today (`/admin`)
Ordered by what is waiting on someone: follow-ups due and replies; reports of the month ready to send; the next prospects; data to fix (unmatched mentions by views, merge suggestions, unrecognised food words, failed runs); the report month's coverage with "finish the month"; spend.

## Data (`/admin/data`, was `/admin/pipeline`)
A month × step grid for a city: what ran, what it found, what failed, what never ran, with the month's posts / mentions / reviews / reports beside it, so a half-finished month is obvious and one click queues what is missing. Runs and Apify costs below.

## Data decisions taken
- Keep the data (venues, brands, reviews, March TikTok history) — wiping would cost money and lose history. Finish **September 2026** with the steps that were cancelled or never ran, so it is the first complete month; October is the month in progress.
- Paid-for data is never thrown away: a review scrape keeps every review from its start date (each belongs to its own month); a pipeline step cut short by a restart goes on where it stopped and re-attaches to its Apify runs (`pipeline-run-orphaned-by-restart`, done).
- TikTok hashtag feeds ignore the actor's date filter (tested 2026-10-18: 20 results from Nov 2025 to Oct 2026, 3 in September), so a month's TikTok posts come mostly from the searches (`publishTime`) and the brand searches. Follow-up: `continuous-listening`.
