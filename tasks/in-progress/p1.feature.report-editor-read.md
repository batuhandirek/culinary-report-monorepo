# Write the report's summary as an editor's read of the month

## Goal
The written part of a report (cover title, headline, findings, actions) is replaced by an editor's read of the month: a letter to the owner in plain prose, a few "worth your attention" cards and "try this" actions, written by one tool-calling model call over the month's material (the posts with their comments, the press, the own account, the worst reviews and what guests noticed changed, each branch's own month, the competitors, the scene, the analysis) rather than over a bag of counts. Today's sections stay underneath, unchanged. The Nhas City Chicken September 2026 report, rebuilt, tells the hype wave, the Cologne queues, the allegation, the turning comments, the delivery failures and the unanswered reviews.

## Why
See `report-overhaul`: the highlights model is asked to summarise metrics with a checklist of findings, so nothing that is not a metric (an allegation, a press wave, a mood turning under the hype, broken delivery) ever reaches the owner, and what does reach them is "9 of 21 written reviews (43%)" and "whatMovedAndWhy says".

## Scope
- In:
  - `ReportEditorAgent` (`gpt-5.4`, the evidence tools of `ReportEvidenceService` through one `ToolRun`, a step and call cap) replacing `ReportHighlightsAgent`; its instructions are the editor's job: read the month, tell the owner what happened and what it means, plainly, in the owner's interest; numbers only from the material; reasons as possibilities; negative press and allegations said as what the post or the press claims; a quiet month said as such; a brand's expansion elsewhere told from press and posts only.
  - The material (`util/editor-pack.ts`, pure): built from the trimmed `ReportData` (`trimForSections` scope kept: the letter never cites a block the report leaves out), the analysis and the month before; organised as material, not metrics: posts by others each with their comments, own posts and accounts, press, reviews (what changed, the critical ones, five stars against the rest as themes with their phrases, counts as plain "n of m"), each place's own month (reviews, complaints, benchmark, search ranks, neighbours, openings), competitors, the scene (the kind of food in the city, rising rows, themes against peers, the baseline), the analysis as given, last month's actions, the calendar, and the data gaps said plainly ("no Google review of September was read; the brand has 3,167 all-time").
  - `ReportHighlights` gains `kind` (`story` | `quiet`), `letter` (paragraphs), `notes` and `trace` (admin only, stripped by `toPublicOutput`); `highlights[]` and `actions[]` keep their names (the mail, the pitch hook, the leave-behind and the portal read them).
  - `util/highlights-check.ts`: the one guard kept: every number in a card or a letter sentence is one the model was given (the pack and every tool answer); the brand's banned words; an input key name. A failing card is dropped, a failing sentence cut; an empty letter gets a code-written fallback.
  - Reviews carry their branch in `ReportData` (`reviews.list[].branch`, `changes[].branch`, `critical[].branch`) for a chain.
  - The view: the letter under the cover, "Worth your attention" and "Try this" with the same cards; the admin report page shows the editor's trace (tool calls, notes, tokens, problems).
  - Tests: the pack (pure), the check (pure), the build with the editor mocked (the existing suites moved to it), the branch on review items.
  - The skill's statements on the highlights agent and the prompt, and `human-docs/04-report-contents.md`.
- Out: the document reshape (sections, appendix, around-you section), the analysis section's own rewrite, the number-word rules in code, German.
