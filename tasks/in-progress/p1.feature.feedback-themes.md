# Feedback themes

## Goal
Every praise and complaint point the LLM extracts from a post or a review carries a `theme` from a fixed vocabulary (~60 themes under the nine topics: `price` → `price_level`, `value_for_money`, `price_increase`, `extra_charges`, `price_other`; …), so "prices went up", "dürüm now €9.50" and "filled rolls are getting more and more expensive" count as three reviews raising `price_increase`. Reports count sources per theme next to the per-topic counts and the highlights prompt is given them. Stored points get a theme through a backfill that reads the details alone, so past months count too without re-extracting posts (which would recreate mentions and lose their matches and corrections).

## Why
Measured on the dev database on 2026-10-21:
- A point is `{ topic, detail }`: one of 9 topics plus a free English phrase. 4,050 review complaint points have 3,816 distinct details (94 % unique); 15,871 praise points have 9,959. Below the topic nothing groups: "expensive" (8), "a bit pricey" (6), "overpriced" (6), "prices have become too high" (1) and "prices are the same despite portions smaller" (1) are all just `price`, and the first three are not the same thing as the last two.
- Reviewer-noticed changes are free strings (355, 324 distinct): "prices went up" ×6, "burgers are getting smaller", "burgers are smaller than before", "made smaller", "half the seating removed", "less space than before". The report groups them by lower-case only (`ReportView.svelte`, `changes`).
- A city-wide "N reviews complained about a price increase" needs a key that is the same in every venue's data. The free detail cannot be summed; the topic is too coarse.
- Prior art: SemEval ABSA (restaurant reviews) labels entity × attribute pairs (FOOD#PRICES, SERVICE#GENERAL); our topic is the entity, the theme the attribute. A closed vocabulary keeps counts comparable month to month and venue to venue. Finding themes the list lacks is `feedback-theme-discovery` (embeddings), not this task.

## Design
- `venues/constants/feedback-themes.constant.ts`: `FEEDBACK_THEMES` (key → `{ topic, label, hint }`), `FEEDBACK_THEME_KEYS`, `feedbackThemeLegend()` for the prompts, `fallbackTheme(topic)` (`food_other`, …, `other`). A theme is what the point is *about*, never its polarity: `portion_size` praised is generous, criticised is small; the list the point sits in says which. Changes against an earlier visit are themes too (`price_increase`, `quality_change`, `ownership_change`, `renovation`, `staff_change`), so "prices went up" is counted as a price increase without a second list.
- `feedbackPointSchema` (shared by `ReviewAnalysisAgent` and `MentionExtractionAgent`) becomes `{ theme, detail }`; the `topic` is derived from the theme in code (`withTopics`, `venues/util/feedback.ts`) so the two never disagree. `FeedbackPoint.theme?` is optional in the interface because stored points from before lack it until backfilled.
- Counting: once per source per theme, like per topic (`countThemes`). `ReportSocialStats`, `ReportReviewStats` and `ReportReviewGroup` gain optional `praiseThemes` / `complaintThemes`, absent in reports built before and **absent while any point of the set has no theme**, so a count is never short. The highlights prompt gets `reviewsCriticisingTheme` etc. as "6 of 34 written reviews" strings keyed "price / price increase" (catch-alls left out), the detail lines carry the theme, and it is told to name a theme with its count when one carries most of a topic.
- Backfill: `FeedbackThemeService` (venues, the owner of reviews) `fileThemes(holders)` sends `{ id, kind, topic, detail }` of points without a theme, 120 a call, to `FeedbackThemeAgent` (mini) and assigns the theme and its topic; `backfillReviews({ cityId?, limit })` for reviews, `MentionExtractionService.backfillThemes` (social) for mentions; `POST /admin/feedback-themes/backfill` (social's controller, which imports venues) runs both and returns what is left. Free (the LLM allowance): 19,921 review points and 6,870 mention points on dev, ~230 calls.
- The legend sits in the agents' instructions (`FEEDBACK_THEMES_INSTRUCTION`), not the schema, where it would repeat for praise and complaints.

## Scope
- In: the above, the tests, the skill.
- Out: showing themes anywhere (the report's topic bars, the Trends page, the portal's topic filter): `city-feedback-themes` does the city view and the report's theme bars together. `Review.changes: string[]` stays as it is (`noticed-changes-as-feedback-points`). Comments' `disagreement` sentence. Themes the list lacks (`feedback-theme-discovery`). A button for the backfill (the Data page's Runs tab is the natural home; the route is enough for now).
