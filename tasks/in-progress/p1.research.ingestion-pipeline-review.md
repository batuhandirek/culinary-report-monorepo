# Review the ingestion pipeline and decide a simpler, gated design

## Goal
Every source is first judged worth digesting or not (cheaply, with a reason), and only what is about eating and
drinking out in the city is read into places and claims; places are only places that serve or sell food or drink
(the post's own author when it is a venue's account); the change flag means one thing and has one writer. The
findings below, measured on dev Berlin, and the decisions taken are the contract for the implementation tasks.

## Findings (dev Berlin, 2026-10-31)

Measured on the 4,000 posts re-read with the scope line (2026-10-30 16:33 → 2026-10-31) and on samples:

1. **No gate: everything gets the full read.** Of 3,695 TikTok posts, 1,084 (29%) named no place and 1,804 (49%)
   none matched to a brand, each at ~2,085 tokens. A sample of 25 with no place: an outfit check, a music track, a
   political speech (8 claims stored), a book café, a lahmacun recipe (5 claims), festival ads in other towns, plus
   real food posts that name no place. Blog feeds of city magazines carry non-dining articles: mushroom foraging
   (`03c50ad0-…`: 11 "places" incl. Grunewald, Tegeler Forst, Loveparade; ~35 claims on where mushrooms grow), supermarket
   deals ("Bio-Angebote KW 21": 4 mentions, 3 matched), picnic recipes. 312 posts hold only other-city mentions.
2. **Places are not restricted to food and drink.** Agent A names parks, forests, districts, museums and events;
   matching then ties some to venues of the same name after dropping generic words: Tiergarten → Café Tiergarten,
   Grunewald → Grunewald, Mitte (llm), Neukölln → CAFÉ NEUKÖLLN, Brandenburger Tor → Hopfingerbräu (llm). Their claims
   count for those brands (Grunewald got a hygiene complaint from "some mushroom lookalikes are poisonous").
3. **A venue's own post has no place.** "3 neue Sorten im Sortiment – kommt vorbei" by a venue's account gave 0
   claims: the post does not name itself, and A only takes places the text names. Own accounts are known
   (`SocialAccount.brand`) but not told to A. Same root as the new-item task (`new-item-announcements`).
4. **The change flag is wrong a third to two thirds of the time.** 1,162 claims flagged (625 posts, 537 reviews). In
   25 positive review changes ~18 were not changes ("delicious as always", "still top-notch", "haven't lost the
   taste", "the other location was fantastic", "came back as soon as I arrived"); in 30 random ones ~11 ("newly
   opened brunch spot", "Berlin was sold out again", "all wraps cost 7€ in April"). Two writers OR'd (A with the
   source, B with the claim alone; `StatementPartsService` only ever sets it), and B reads "again" as a change. It
   feeds the report's "what guests noticed changed", the theme pages' Changes side and the lead score.
5. **Scope line helped, not enough.** TikTok claims about nothing fell from 37% to ~21%; a sample still holds
   chatter ("replacing all the toilets cost about $60", "first episode of Chef's Secret") and non-claims about the
   city ("Berlin", "Berlin food", "the videos are still from Berlin").
6. **Agent B on reviews is good.** 25 random review claims: aspects and foods right, one sentiment wrong ("the meat
   falls apart": negative). Reviews need no redesign beyond the change flag.
7. **Prompt sizes are not the problem.** `PostClaimsAgent` ~1.8k characters plus the food topic labels; the tokens are
   the input (transcripts, articles). What is overloaded is the job: one call decides relevance, places, seven
   fields a place, and every claim.

## Proposed design (to decide)

- **Stage 0, code, free:** a post with no text and no transcript, or hashtags only and no transcript, is not digested
  (`no_content`).
- **Stage 1, the gate (new, cheap):** one mini call per ~20 posts on the title or caption and the first ~600
  characters (~150 tokens a post): `digest` or `skip` with a reason (`not_food`: music, fashion, politics…;
  `home_cooking`: recipes; `groceries`: supermarket deals; `outdoors`: foraging, picnics; `other_city`). Stored on the
  post (`digest` + `skipReason` + `gatedAt`), shown on the post page and the Claims tab, re-gated on a prompt change.
  A skipped post gets no places and no claims ("not digested", the user's call). Later candidate: Cloudflare's
  Clef Flash, a decision model, for this question (see the conversation of 2026-10-31).
- **Stage 2, agent A:** places are only places that serve or sell food or drink (restaurant, café, bar, bakery,
  food truck, market stall, food hall, shop); a park, forest, district, street, museum, event or organisation is
  not one, a claim about it is about the city or nothing. A post by a venue's own account is told so (`ownBrand` in
  the context) and its claims are about that place without it being named.
- **The change flag:** one definition ("something is now different from before at this place: new owner, prices
  went up, smaller portions, renovated, a new dish or menu, worse or better than last time"; not consistency, a
  return visit, another branch, an opening (that is `isNewOpening`), a wish, something that changed during the
  visit), in A and B, and **B's answer is final** (set, not only added), so a prompt change is fixed by re-reading the
  claims, not the sources.
- **Existing data:** the new A re-reads every post (gate first); a stored mention the answer doesn't name and that
  is unmatched (`none`, `other_city`) is deleted with its claims; a matched one is listed for an admin (Today) rather
  than deleted; `manual` and `ignored` never touched. Re-gated skipped posts lose their mentions and claims unless a
  mention is `manual`.
- **Cost of the re-run** (dev Berlin, 5,146 posts): gate ~0.8M tokens, A on the ~70% digested ~7.2M, B ~2M: ~10M
  mini, one night at 10M, no money within the allowance. Reviews: only B over the ~540 flagged changes (~40k).
