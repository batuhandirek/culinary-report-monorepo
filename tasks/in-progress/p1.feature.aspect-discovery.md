# Aspect discovery

## Goal
A one-off boot job groups each city's loose claims (no aspect, or filed at a root) by meaning, names each
group, creates the aspects the tree lacks (no review step, as for food topics), and has agent B read the
claims near each new aspect again so they move into it.

## Why
- ~30k of dev Berlin's ~68k claims sit at a root or under no aspect. "Missing aspects" needed an admin to
  click "Create aspect", and its 0.82 threshold left 5,014 of October's 6,000 claims alone (24 groups).
- A group that justified an aspect never counted under it until B re-read its claims (the absorbed task).

## Scope
- In: `AspectDiscoveryService` (`discover`, `runAll`), job `aspect-discovery` queued weekly by
  `AspectDiscoveryCron` (the boot switch is gone: it ran on production 2026-11-03), floors in `constants/aspect-discovery.constant.ts`,
  `AspectSuggestionAgent.isAspect`, the shared loose-claims SQL. Explore's groups stay untitled: they are worked out per view, so a title would be an LLM call per view.
- Out: re-filing claims an *existing* aspect fits (B saw it and chose the root: a B prompt fix, scored by the
  eval); an "aspect view" embedding with
  dish words masked (~$0.08, ask first); showing `isAspect` on the Missing aspects tab.
