# Claim model v2

**Do this before the posts' re-read runs** (`reread-sources-with-new-agents`, the backlog `reread-posts`, ~10M tokens):
agent A must write the quote in that one pass, or the posts are read twice.

## Decisions (2026-10-31, the user)
1. **Quote + English.** Every claim keeps the source's exact words: `Statement.quote`, a verbatim substring of the
   source in its language ("Die Pommes waren matschig"), beside `text`, the English rendering ("the Pommes were
   soggy"). Agent A writes both; agent B reads both; the admin shows both; the reference set (`claims-eval-set`)
   matches on the quote. A quote that is not a substring of the source (after whitespace folding) is dropped (null),
   never invented; transcripts are machine text and a quote of them is what they say.
2. **A `kind` beside the aspect**, one per claim, set by agent B (refillable without re-reading sources):
   `opinion` (a judgement), `fact` (neutral information: "fries 10 €", "open until 22"), `change` (something now
   different at the place: `CHANGE_RULE`), `new_item` (a dish, drink or menu added: "meet our new Mint & Ginger hot
   drink"), `deal` (a limited offer: dates, hours or a condition: "baklava 20 € a kilo, 15–25 March"), `event` (an
   anniversary, a pop-up, a party, a live night), `question` (asked, not said: comments), `verdict` (a recommendation
   either way: "highly recommend", "avoid at all costs", "will be back"). It replaces `Statement.change` (dropped;
   `kind = change`); sentiment stays its own axis (a deal is usually neutral, a verdict positive or negative).
3. **The change aspects fold in.** `price_increase`, `quality_change`, `ownership_change` (and any other leaf that only
   says "changed") go: their parts are re-pointed by SQL to the base aspect (`price_level`; the quality aspect B
   would pick, else the root `food`; ownership to the aspect the tree gives it, else the root `service` or `other`, decided
   when listing them) and their claims get `kind = change`, no LLM. Aspects only say what a claim is about. Links to a
   folded aspect (theme pages, reports' `praiseThemes` / `complaintThemes` keys, `ReportData.aspects`) move; stored
   reports are rebuilt or read with a fallback.
4. **The second opinion layer goes.** `Review.sentiment`, `Mention.sentiment` and `Mention.summary` are dropped:
   nothing counted by topic may use them, and what used them reads claims. A review's overall view is its stars. A
   mention's (or post's) sentiment where a reader needs one (the report's posts sentiment split, the lead score's
   negative posts, the portal series, the editor pack) is derived from its claims at read time (all judged claims
   positive → positive, all negative → negative, both → mixed, none judged → neutral), one SQL helper and one pure
   function, never stored. Where a summary was shown (the report's and editor pack's posts, the brand Overview's top
   posts, the front page's praise phrases, the admin post page), the mention's claims about the place are shown, the
   quote first. Agent A no longer answers `sentiment` or `summary` for a place, nor a review's sentiment.

## Order of work
1. Entity changes: `Statement.quote` (text, null), `Statement.kind` (enum `statement_kind`, null until B reads it),
   drop `Statement.change` (migrate: `kind = 'change'` where `change`), drop `Review.sentiment`, `Mention.sentiment`,
   `Mention.summary`. Hand-written data migrations where the generator would lose data (backend-entity).
2. Agents: A (`ReviewClaimsAgent`, `PostClaimsAgent`) answers `quote` per claim and loses the overall fields; B
   (`ClaimPartsAgent`) answers `kind` with the definitions above (one constant like `CHANGE_RULE`, e.g. `KIND_RULES`),
   reads quote and text, and stays final on it; `CHANGE_RULE` becomes the `change` kind's definition.
3. The aspect fold (decision 3): list the leaves, re-point by SQL, set kinds, remove the rows; the legend and the
   aspect seed follow.
4. Readers: every reader of `change` (`not s.change` in SQL, `changesOf`, `groupChanges`, the theme pages' Changes side,
   the lead score's "noticed changes", the editor pack) reads `kind = 'change'`; every reader of the dropped
   sentiment and summary reads the claims (decision 4); the Claims tables get a Kind column and filter; the post page
   and the review lists show the quote.
5. Backfill without sources: B over every stored claim for `kind` (clear `partsAt`; the nightly `statement-parts`,
   ~60k claims, ~4M mini tokens: put the estimate to the user first). Quotes arrive with re-reads only: posts with the
   pending re-read (move its cutoff, `POSTS_GATED_AT`, to this change's time), reviews with a `reread-reviews` pass at a
   new cutoff (~4M tokens, asked first).
6. Fold the tasks this absorbs: `new-item-announcements` and `deal-announcements` become filters (`kind = new_item`,
   `kind = deal`, with their food parts and amounts) plus whatever list they still need; `claims-extraction-fixes` step
   3 loses verdict, events and deals (kinds now) and keeps loyalty and hype if the clusters show them;
   `consolidate-topic-vocabularies` step 1 makes comment questions claims of `kind = question`.
7. Tests (PGlite: quote kept only when it is a substring; kinds; the fold moving parts and counts; derived sentiment;
   every moved reader), `create:client`, `svelte-check`, `backend:test`, the skill (the statements rule, the aspects
   rule, the change rule, the readers).

## Scope
- In: the above.
- Out: an opening as a claim kind (`Mention.isNewOpening` and its corroboration stay as they are; a later decision),
  Google tags as statements (phase 5, they get `kind = fact` when written), the Explore page (`talked-about-pages`
  adds a Kind filter once this lands).
