# Decouple reading from scraping, and track every venue's and big creator's figures

## Goal
Apify scrapes never fail or stop because an LLM is out: they store their rows, and a reading job
reads whatever is unread as soon as a model answers. Steps that need what the reading found
wait for it instead of failing. Every open venue's Google rating and review count is read
weekly, and every brand account's and big creator's followers and post count every two weeks.

## Why
On the cloned production DB (2026-11-04): a failed step stops the whole `MONTHLY` sequence, so an
LLM outage on the 2nd would lose the month's paid review, delivery, comments and press scrapes;
reading is only retried at the next cron (a day later); an OpenAI account refused at 16:47 is
blocked for 24 h in memory, so the next 04:00 read fails even after the allowance reset at
00:00 UTC. Only ~4,000 of 9,503 open venues get rating snapshots (the same ones each time), and a
brand account's followers are read once and never again; creators' only when a post of theirs
turns up.

## Scope
- In:
  1. **OpenAI block length by reason** (`OpenAIAccountsService`): quota/allowance → until the next
     00:00 UTC; "no credits" → `LLM_CREDIT_RETRY_MINUTES` (60); a rejected key → `LLM_BLOCK_HOURS`.
  2. **The reading job** (`IngestionService`, pipeline module): per city, under an in-process lock,
     extraction (gate + agent A) until nothing is unread, matching (+ account matching when anything
     changed), the city's unread reviews (newest first, capped per pass), comments, press. A
     provider failure stops it quietly (`blocked`), never throws. Queued (`pipeline-ingest`,
     stately per city) after every scrape run that stores sources, and hourly by `IngestionCron`.
     The steps `EXTRACT_MENTIONS`, `MATCH_MENTIONS`, `ANALYSE_REVIEWS`, `ANALYSE_COMMENTS` stay for
     an admin's run, under the same lock, and leave every cron and `MONTHLY`.
  3. **Gated steps wait** (`PipelineRunStatusEnum.WAITING`, `PipelineRun.waitingSince`): a step
     that reads the reading's results runs the reading inline first; when it can't catch up (no
     model) the run waits and is woken after every reading pass. Steps reading posts and matches
     (lookups, venue social, profiles, competitors, reviews, details, delivery, comments, press,
     search ranks, own-account lookups) go on after `READ_WAIT_HOURS` (24) with what is read;
     reports and lead scores wait until everything is read. `params.noWait` skips the gate.
  4. **`MONTHLY` without reading steps**; stored monthly results re-keyed by a migration.
  5. **Crons**: the daily Instagram run and the weekly listening queue scrapes only (lookups and
     own-account lookups gated); a cron never queues a step it queued for the city within hours.
  6. **Ratings weekly for every open venue** (`SNAPSHOT_RATINGS`: Tuesdays, every open venue, talked
     about first; ~$5.70 a run in Berlin).
  7. **`TRACK_PROFILES`** (weekly cron, each account due 13 days after its last profile snapshot, so
     every two weeks): profile-only scrapes of every brand account with an open branch in the city
     and every creator account of the city with `TRACK_CREATOR_MIN_FOLLOWERS` (5,000) followers or
     `TRACK_CREATOR_MIN_VIEWS` (10,000) average views over its stored posts, plus once each creator
     account whose followers are unknown (Instagram posts carry none). ~$7 a week.
- Out (follow-ups, see Log): weekly review text for report brands (the monthly scrape pays from the
  month's start, so a weekly one needs per-venue "since"), weekly comments and press, venue social
  weekly, reading job progress on the Data page beyond the waiting state, finding venues' accounts.
