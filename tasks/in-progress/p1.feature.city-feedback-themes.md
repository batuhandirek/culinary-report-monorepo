# City-wide feedback themes

## Goal
A Trends tab "Themes" for a city and month: per theme, how many written reviews and how many posts by others raised it (praise and complaints apart), across how many venues, its share against the month before, the venues it comes up at most, and sample details. The report's city section compares the venue's complaint-theme shares with the median of the month's report venues ("a price increase was criticised in 18 % of your written reviews; 4 % at the month's report venues"), and the report's topic bars open into themes.

## Why
- Today praise and complaints are counted per venue only (`summarizeReport`); nothing sums them across venues. With `feedback-themes` every point carries a comparable key, so the sum is one query.
- It is the city-level story the public pieces and the pitch need ("Berlin's complaint of the month: card payments"), and a venue's share only means something next to the city's.
- Reviews exist only for report venues and their competitors (the top ~20 brands and their picks), so "across the city" for reviews means those venues; posts cover every matched mention. The tab must say so (coverage next to each count, as the Topics tab does for mentions).

## Design
- `trends/services/feedback-theme-insight.service.ts`: `city(cityId, month)` → `{ themes: ThemeRow[], coverage }`, one SQL over `reviews` (joined to venues of the city, written, the month, points unnested with `jsonb_array_elements`) and one over `mentions` (matched, the post's city, own posts out via `ownPostSql`), counted in the pure `trends/util/theme-counts.ts`: a source counts once per theme and polarity; `share` = sources raising it / written reviews (or posts by others) with any point; delta in points against the month before; `venues` = distinct brands; `topBrands` (three, by sources); `samples` (five distinct details). Points without a theme yet (stored before themes, backfill not run) are counted apart as "not filed yet", never under a catch-all, the way a report's theme counts are absent rather than short.
- `GET /admin/feedback-themes?cityId=&month=` (trends module's admin controller, next to topic insights), DTOs with the theme key, label and topic.
- Frontend: `TrendsThemesTab` on `/admin/trends` (a table per polarity, filter by topic, sort by sources or delta, a row opens its samples and top brands; coverage line); `TopicBars` in `ReportView` gains the themes under each topic bar when the data has them.
- Report: `ReportCitySection.themes?` (`{ key, label, venueShare, median, venues }` for complaint themes with 5+ written reviews at the venue), built with the baseline in `report-city.service.ts`; one sentence in the highlights prompt.

## Scope
- In: the above; tests.
- Out: the portal (an owner sees the venue's own themes in the report; a city comparison there is a later decision), public pieces (`monthly-city-highlights-page` can quote the tab), a theme's own page.
