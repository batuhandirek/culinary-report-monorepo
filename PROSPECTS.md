# Prospects: Berlin, October 2026

20 venues likely to pay for a monthly report, ranked by how warm the pitch is. Picked from the mentions, posts and Google reviews in the dev database on 16 October 2026.

**What I looked for:**
- **Lives on social:** buzz from other creators in the last two months.
- **Already spends on it:** they run their own TikTok or Instagram, or pay creators or Neotaste for posts.
- **Has a problem we can name:** visible in the reviews or posts.
- **Independent:** 1–3 locations, with an owner who decides.

**Left out:** chains, institutions and anyone who doesn't need help: Markthalle Neun, REWE, Mustafa's, Rüyam, Curry 36, Hofbräu, and fine dining with PR agencies.

## How to sell it

- **Free sample:** send #1–4 their own report as the pitch, since a real insight about their own venue sells better than a demo. Only #1–4 already have a full Google review analysis.
- **Others need a run first:** the rest need the reviews step before their reports are as sharp. That costs about $0.10–0.30 in Apify credit per venue.

## Caveats

- **Incomplete data:** mention counts only cover posts our searches found.
- **Reply rates:** I only have them for venues that already have a report.
- **No website:** annelies and DREI. have no website listed (bite. doesn't either), so you'd reach them by phone or social media.
