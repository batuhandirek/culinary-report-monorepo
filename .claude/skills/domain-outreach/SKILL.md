# Outreach

Selling the report to venues, by hand: the admin writes from their own mailbox (Gmail), phone or Instagram, and the app keeps the record. The mails the app sends itself go to a venue that subscribed: the monthly report (`ReportDeliveryService`) and the weekly watch's critical-review alert (`ReviewAlertService`).

## Map

- Backend `modules/outreach`: `Prospect` (one per brand, `OneToOne` with cascade, whatever its cities; `stage`, `language`, contact fields, `emailConsentAt`, `hook`, `notes`, `nextFollowUpAt`, and `lastTouchAt` / `outreachCount` kept from the touches), `OutreachTouch` (one log entry: `kind`, `at`, `subject`, `body`, `template`, `stage` for a stage move; for a VISIT or CALL also `outcome`, `comeBackAt`, `emailConsent`; for a touch that sent a report, `report` and `share`, the link it sent), `constants/outreach.constant.ts` (`ProspectStageEnum`, `OutreachTouchKindEnum`, `OutreachTouchOutcomeEnum`, `OUTGOING_TOUCH_KINDS`, `IN_PERSON_TOUCH_KINDS`, `CLOSED_STAGES`, `FOLLOW_UP_DAYS`, `PROSPECT_LANGUAGES`).
- `OutreachService` is the one entry point: `add`, `update` (details, stage, follow-up), `log`, `removeTouch`, `remove`, `summary`, `writeHook`. Routes in `admin.outreach.controller.ts` under `/admin/outreach` (`summary`, `prospects` (filter `brandId`, `q` on the brand name, `cityId` = a branch there; sortable by the brand's `leadScore`), `prospects/:id`, `prospects/:id/touches`, `prospects/:id/hook`, `suggested`, `reports/:reportId/send`). A mutation answers with the whole prospect (`getProspect`), touches newest first and the brand's newest report cut down to what an email quotes (`latestReport`, via `ReportService.latest`). A prospect shows as its brand (`brand`: name, score, open branches) and its place (`venue`: the most reviewed open branch, the phone, website and address the composer uses; `city` its city), mapped in `util/prospect-output.ts`, which the overview reuses.
- `ProspectQueryService` answers the read-only questions other modules ask, so they can import outreach without its mutations: `wonBrandIds` (the pipeline reports on those every month, `PipelineService.topBrands`), `instagramHandles(brandIds)` (the prospects' `instagram`, which `SCRAPE_ACCOUNTS` links as the brand's own account before scraping the watched profiles), `suggested(size)` (the best brand lead scores not on the list yet, via `BrandLeadScoreService.ranked`) and `due` (follow-ups due today). The pipeline imports `OutreachModule`; outreach never imports the pipeline.
- Frontend: selling has no page of its own; it is the brand's. The sales list is the Brands page's **Sales list** and **Customers** segments (`/admin/brands?segment=prospects|customers`, filter by stage, sort by next follow-up; `brands/components/SalesSummary` tiles above, linking to stage filters) and its **Suggested prospects** tab (`brands/components/SuggestedProspects`: the top 20 by lead score with the signals, one-click add and "Add all", one add at a time since each may write a hook). One prospect is worked on the brand page's **Sales** tab (`brands/[brandId]/components/BrandSalesTab`: `ProspectStageSelect`, `ProspectWorkspace` without the report panel — the Reports tab is next to it — and the brand's `LeadScorePanel`); "Add to sales" is in the brand header; `stores/BrandProspectStore` keeps the brand's prospect for the header, the Overview's Sales card and the tab. `components/admin/outreach/` holds the working set (`ProspectWorkspace`: the notices, then `ProspectComposer`, `ProspectLog`, `ProspectDetails` + `EditProspectDialog`, `ProspectReport`, `ProspectPortal`: who has the brand's portal, invite and take off through `POST/DELETE /admin/outreach/prospects/:id/portal-members`, the mail in the prospect's language; `BrandMemberService` does the work). Today lists due follow-ups and replies. There is no outreach page: the API keeps its `/admin/outreach` routes. Wording and moves: `lib/outreach-status.ts` (`TOUCH_OUTCOMES`, `IN_PERSON_KINDS`, `needsEmailConsent` too); badge colours: `prospectStageVariant` in `admin-format.ts`.
- `ReportDeliveryService` mails a `WON` prospect with a `contactEmail` its report (template `ReportReadyEmail` in `packages/emails`, a link of its own from `ReportShareService.create`, logged as an EMAIL touch with template `report-ready` linked to the report and that share, stamped on `Report.sentAt`): `deliverBuilt` (the brand's newest report of that city) from `ReportBuiltWorker` on the reports module's `report-built` event, `sendNow` from `POST /admin/outreach/reports/:reportId/send` (the E-mail panel on `/admin/reports/[reportId]`).
- `ReviewAlertService` (from `ReviewAlertWorker` on the venues module's `review-alert` event) mails a `WON` prospect with a `contactEmail` one critical review of any of its brand's branches (`ReviewAlertEmail`, the prospect's language; a chain's branch named with its street) with a `ReviewReplyAgent` draft (exported by `ReportsModule`, saved on `Review.replyDraft`), a button to the venue's Google Maps page and a link to the brand portal's Reviews tab; logged as an EMAIL touch with template `review-alert`. No longer `WON` or no address: nothing is sent.
- Templates: `constants/OUTREACH_TEMPLATES.ts` (copy in `en` and `de`, the variable syntax in its header), filled by `lib/outreach-template.ts` (`fillTemplate`, `outreachVars`, `suggestedTemplateKey`, Gmail and mailto links). No city is written into copy: `{city}` is the place's `City.name` (`AdminProspectOutputDto.city`); `{venue}` is the brand's name.

## Rules

- **A brand is one prospect, whatever its cities**: `add(brandId)` twice returns the one prospect; a won brand gets a report in every city it has branches in, each mailed by itself. Merging brands with two prospects is refused (`BrandService.merge`, 409); merging moves the one prospect to the chain.
- **Stages move by what is logged.** An outgoing touch (email, DM, call, visit) moves `TO_CONTACT` to `CONTACTED` and counts a try; a `REPLY` moves `TO_CONTACT`/`CONTACTED` to `REPLIED`. Any other move is the admin's, through `update`, and leaves a `STAGE` touch in the log. `log` refuses `STAGE`.
- **The follow-up sequence runs only while we wait for a first answer** (`CONTACTED`): `FOLLOW_UP_DAYS` (4, then 7) after the first and second try, none after the third (the page then suggests Lost or a later date). A date given with the touch wins; `null` means none; a visit's or call's `comeBackAt` wins over both. A reply clears the follow-up unless it carries one ("later" → a date months out). Moving to a closed stage clears it. "Due" means by the end of today, UTC (`dueBefore`).
- **A visit or a call is logged with what happened**: `outcome` (`OutreachTouchOutcomeEnum`: spoke to the owner, to staff, nobody there, closed), `comeBackAt` ("the owner is in on Tuesdays after 15:00", which becomes `nextFollowUpAt`) and `emailConsent` (they said we may e-mail them). `log` refuses any of the three on another kind (400), so a NOTE never carries an outcome. An outcome never moves the stage: "spoke to the owner, said no" is the salesperson's move to Lost. The composer treats the CALL and VISIT templates as scripts: the script is read, and the notes, outcome, come-back time and consent are logged with the touch.
- **Consent to e-mail is a date on the prospect** (`Prospect.emailConsentAt`): a visit or call logged with `emailConsent` stamps it with the touch's time; `update` sets it by hand (an OK that came in writing) or clears it (`null`); deleting the touch leaves it, since it is the admin's record. The composer warns before an EMAIL to a prospect with no consent and no logged REPLY (`needsEmailConsent`): the send and log buttons stay behind "I have their OK". The report mail (`ReportDeliveryService`) does not read it: it goes to WON prospects only, who agreed by subscribing.
- **The leave-behind is the report's cover on one A5 page** (`GET /admin/reports/:id/leave-behind.pdf`): the cover title, headline, three findings (`LEAVE_BEHIND_FINDINGS`), the brand's name, the printing admin's name and e-mail (and mobile when set), and a QR code to the report's live share link (`ReportShareService.ensureShare`, so a scan is counted as an open on the same share, print after print). The QR is an SVG drawn on the server with `uqr` (`reports/util/leave-behind.ts`, no network) and handed with the rest (`LeaveBehindData`) to the frontend's `/print/leave-behind` page on `window.__LEAVE_BEHIND__`, printed by `ReportPdfService.renderLeaveBehind` like the report. "Print leave-behind" is a row action on the brand's Reports tab and a button on the composer's visit template; it needs a report.
- **`WON` means a paying subscriber**: the monthly run scrapes and reports on its venue whatever its buzz rank (`PipelineService.topBrands`). `LOST` and `OPTED_OUT` venues still get a report when they rank; only outreach stops.
- **`OPTED_OUT` is permanent record**: no outgoing touch and no follow-up date for it, and `remove` refuses it, so nobody writes to them again. Notes and replies can still be logged.
- **Notes don't count**: a `NOTE` changes no stage, count or `lastTouchAt`. Deleting a touch recounts `outreachCount` and `lastTouchAt` but never moves the stage back.
- **A touch that sends a report links it, with a link of its own** (`OutreachTouch.report` / `share`, both set null when they go): the report mail creates a share per send, and the composer's "Add a report link" on a template that carries the report (`attachReport`) makes a fresh share through `POST /admin/reports/:id/shares`, puts its URL in the message and logs the touch with `reportId` / `shareId` while the URL is still in it. `log` refuses another brand's report and a link of another report (400). The log shows per such touch "opened N×, last …" or "not opened" from the share's own counter, so the admin's copied link, the leave-behind's QR (`ensureShare`) and each mail count apart.
- **Template keys are stored on every touch** (`template`) to compare which copy gets answers: give rewritten copy a new key rather than reusing one. Templates live in the frontend because only the composer fills them.
- **The hook is written in the prospect's `language`**, since templates paste it as is. Reports are English, so the German templates say so.
- **An empty hook is written by `PitchHookAgent`** (`PitchHookService.suggest`: one call from the brand's newest report and `Brand.leadSignals`' raw inputs, a chain's branch count instead of a district; no call when it has neither) when a prospect is added without one, and again on "Suggest a hook" (`writeHook`, which overwrites). A failed call on `add` leaves the hook empty rather than failing the add. The prompt asks for one sentence of 12–20 words (25 at most) from the data only; stored report data may predate a field, so `pitchHookInput` reads every one as optional.
- **Cold email to businesses in Germany needs their prior consent (UWG §7 Abs. 2 Nr. 3)**; a B2B phone call needs only presumed consent. The call template asks "Can I email it?" for that reason, and every first email offers a way out. Keep both when rewriting copy.

- **A report is mailed by itself once**: the build event sends only when `Report.sentAt` is empty and the report is the venue's newest, so a rebuild or a backfilled month mails nobody; "Send now" sends again. Only `WON` with an address, never another stage (selling stays by hand). The recipient has no account, so no preference switch applies: the mail says to reply "stop", which the admin records as `OPTED_OUT`. The mail is queued before the touch is logged, so a failure repeats it rather than losing it.
