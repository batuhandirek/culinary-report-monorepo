01
Luna RamenFriedrichshain · Boxhagener Straße

Berlin's longest ramen queue, and nobody seems to mind. The tonkotsu is what everyone filmed; the spicy miso is what everyone came back for. Half the city says "worth it", the other half is still in line.

Dish of the moment: tonkotsu

Café KiezbrotNeukölln · Weserstraße

The Sunday brunch plate that Neukölln has quietly made its default. People talk about the sourdough, the egg that's always right, and the fact that you can still get a table if you come before ten.

What Berlin's actually eating.

Four dishes the city couldn't put down this month, and where it's getting them.

Dish of the Month Everywhere

Pistachio künefe

It started as a dessert in Neukölln and ended up on menus in Mitte. The crunch-and-stretch clip is October's most repeated shot. Best versions: Pfeffer & Salz, Yüksel Mangal, and a Kreuzberg bakery that only makes it on Fridays.

Still rising

Tonkotsu ramen

Friedrichshain's dish of the autumn. The broth matters, the queue is part of the story, and Luna Ramen owns both.

Breakfast

Mohnschnecke

Berlin woke up early this month. The poppy-seed swirl from Bäckerei Mohn is the reason Wedding has its first place in the top five.

Late night

Adana, extra scharf

Sonnenallee after midnight. The skewer is the order, the chilli is the dare, and the ayran is non-negotiable.

Three things Berlin decided this month.

Trends · Oct 2026
01

Sonnenallee is the street.

Three of October's top ten sit on one Neukölln street. Berlin isn't going out for Turkish food any more; it's going to Sonnenallee and deciding there.

02

Brunch moved north.

For the first time, Wedding out-talked Prenzlauer Berg on Sunday mornings. The plates are smaller, the queues shorter, the sourdough just as good.

03

The queue is the content.

Berlin filmed waiting in line more than it filmed the food. A 40-minute ramen queue is now a recommendation, not a warning.

New in Town · this week

Just opened. Already talked about.

Three openings Berlin found before the reviews did.

Haus PhoMitte · Torstraße · opened Thursday

A pho counter with eight seats and a broth that people are already calling the city's best. Expect a line by the weekend.

New
Tuna & SonsKreuzberg · Oranienstraße · opened last week

Fish sandwiches out of a former Späti. The balık ekmek is the one Kreuzberg is posting; the pickled onions are the one it's arguing about.

New
Kiosk BrotSchöneberg · Akazienstraße · opened Saturday

A sourdough window with no seats and no menu beyond what came out of the oven. Schöneberg has been waiting for exactly this.
